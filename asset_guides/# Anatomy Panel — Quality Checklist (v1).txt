# Anatomy Panel — Quality Checklist (v1)

## Core Principle

An anatomy panel is a **measurement input**, not a final image.

Every requirement should support:

- geometric consistency
- clean silhouette extraction
- reliable scaling and alignment

---

# 1. Framing & Camera (CRITICAL)

## Required

- Full body visible (head → feet)
- No cropping anywhere
- Camera is:
  - Straight-on (no tilt)
  - Neutral perspective (minimal distortion)
- Consistent camera distance across all panels

## Must NOT happen

- Zoom differences between views
- Perspective exaggeration (wide lens effect)
- Camera tilt (up/down angle)

---

# 2. Aspect Ratio & Scale

## Required

- Image ratio: **2:3 (width:height)**
- Subject occupies:
  - ~75–85% of image height
  - Does NOT touch edges

## Must NOT happen

- Subject too small
- Subject touching top/bottom edges
- Inconsistent subject size across panels

---

# 3. Pose (EXTREMELY IMPORTANT)

## Required

- Neutral standing pose
- Weight evenly distributed
- Arms:
  - Relaxed
  - Slightly separated from torso (visible gap)
- Legs:
  - Hip-width apart
- Spine:
  - Natural upright posture

## Must NOT happen

- Contrapposto (weight shift)
- Bent knees
- Arms touching torso
- Asymmetry between views

---

# 4. View Accuracy

## FRONT

- Perfectly symmetrical
- Shoulders level
- Hips level

## SIDE

- True profile (90°)
- No torso twist toward camera
- Spine curvature clearly readable

## BACK

- Matches front alignment
- No rotation
- Shoulders and hips symmetrical

## Must NOT happen

- 3/4 leakage into side/back views
- Rotated torso
- Inconsistent stance between views

---

# 5. Background & Separation

## Required

- Flat, uniform background
- Neutral light color (off-white / light gray)
- Strong contrast between subject and background

## Must NOT happen

- Gradients
- Textures
- Background shadows
- Colors similar to subject

---

# 6. Silhouette Quality (VERY IMPORTANT)

## Required

- Clean, continuous outline
- No holes in silhouette
- Clear separation:
  - Arms from torso
  - Legs from each other

## Must NOT happen

- Merged limbs (legs touching, arms fused to torso)
- Blurry edges
- Noise interfering with silhouette

---

# 7. Lighting

## Required

- Soft, even lighting
- Low contrast
- No harsh shadows
- No directional drama

## Must NOT happen

- Strong side lighting
- Cast shadows on background
- Blown highlights or crushed shadows

---

# 8. Clothing

## Required

- Minimal, form-revealing clothing
- Consistent across all views

Examples:

- Fitted shorts
- Compression shorts
- Tight shirt or bare torso

## Must NOT happen

- Loose clothing
- Layered outfits
- Fabric obscuring body shape

---

# 9. Proportion Consistency (Across Panels)

## Required

- Same height across all views
- Same limb length
- Same shoulder width
- Same hip width
- Same head size

## Must NOT happen

- Limb length changes between views
- Torso compression/expansion
- Head scale drifting

---

# 10. Identity Neutrality

## Required

- Neutral expression
- No stylization emphasis
- No dramatic posing

## Must NOT happen

- Hero poses
- Stylized exaggeration
- Aesthetic bias

---

# 11. Assembler Compatibility (CRITICAL)

Before using a panel, verify:

- Clean silhouette can be extracted
- Lowest pixel = feet (no noise below)
- Highest pixel = head/hair
- No stray pixels outside main body
- No background artifacts

---

# Quick Validation Checklist

Use this before accepting a panel:

- [ ] Full body visible
- [ ] Arms separated from torso
- [ ] Legs separated
- [ ] Neutral pose
- [ ] Flat background
- [ ] Consistent scale vs other views
- [ ] No perspective distortion
- [ ] Clean silhouette

---

# Common Failure Patterns

## 1. Over-stylized lighting

Looks good, breaks detection

## 2. Arms touching torso

Breaks silhouette separation

## 3. Inconsistent scaling

Different height between views

## 4. Soft or blurry edges

Reduces mask accuracy

## 5. Subtle pose drift

Small stance differences across views

---

# Summary

A valid anatomy panel should function as:

> A clean silhouette + reliable bounding box for measurement

Not:

- a stylized render
- a composition-focused image
