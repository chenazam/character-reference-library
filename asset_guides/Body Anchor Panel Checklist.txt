# Body Anchor Panel Checklist (v1)

## Purpose

Body anchor panels define the **natural, character-aware full-body appearance** while remaining structurally locked to the anatomy sheet.

They introduce controlled realism without altering proportions.

---

## Input References (REQUIRED)

### Primary
- Anatomy sheet (strict geometry source)

### Secondary
- Face anchor (identity consistency)

### Optional
- Character description / style metadata

### Rules
- Do NOT use previous body anchors as input
- Do NOT chain generations (no body → body)

---

## 1. Framing & Camera

- Full body visible (head to feet)
- Consistent framing across all views
- Same camera distance as anatomy panels
- Neutral perspective (no distortion)

---

## 2. Pose (Controlled Naturalism)

### Required
- Upright posture
- Spine neutral (no slouch, no stiffness)
- Pose consistent across all views

### Allowed
- Very slight natural relaxation

### Not Allowed
- Contrapposto
- Weight shift
- Bent knees
- Pose variation between views

---

## 3. Arms & Hands

### Required
- Arms hanging downward
- Elbows mostly extended
- Clear separation from torso

### Hands
- Neutral rotation
- Fingers relaxed but controlled

### Not Allowed
- Arms touching torso
- Large pose variation between views

---

## 4. Legs & Feet

### Required
- Legs mostly straight
- Feet placement consistent across views

### Allowed
- Minimal natural softness

### Not Allowed
- Visible weight shift
- Hip tilt
- Stance variation

---

## 5. Clothing (Identity Layer)

### Required
- Consistent across all views
- Character-appropriate
- Preserves silhouette readability

### Recommended
- Fitted or semi-fitted clothing
- Minimal fabric distortion

### Not Allowed
- Bulky clothing
- Layered outfits
- Shape-obscuring garments

---

## 6. Silhouette Integrity

### Required
- Clear readable outline
- Visible:
  - shoulder width
  - waist shape
  - hip structure
  - leg proportions

### Not Allowed
- Limb merging
- Silhouette ambiguity

---

## 7. Cross-View Consistency

### Required
- Identical pose across:
  - front
  - side
  - three-quarter

- Clothing consistency across views
- Body proportions match exactly

### Not Allowed
- Pose reinterpretation per view
- Clothing variation between views

---

## 8. Identity Expression

### Required
- Reflects character personality
- Matches face anchor identity

### Allowed
- Subtle natural posture nuance

### Not Allowed
- Stylization that alters proportions
- Exaggerated posing

---

## 9. Lighting

### Required
- Soft, neutral, even lighting

### Not Allowed
- Dramatic shadows
- Directional lighting that obscures form

---

## 10. Validation Checklist

### Geometry
- [ ] Matches anatomy proportions
- [ ] No limb length drift
- [ ] No torso distortion

### Pose
- [ ] Same pose across all views
- [ ] Arms separated from torso
- [ ] No weight shift

### Clothing
- [ ] Consistent across views
- [ ] Does not distort body shape

### Silhouette
- [ ] Clean readable outline
- [ ] No merged limbs

---

## Key Rule

Body anchor = controlled natural deformation of anatomy

Silhouette difference vs anatomy:
~90–95% identical
