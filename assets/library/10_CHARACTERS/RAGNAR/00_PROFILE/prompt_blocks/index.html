    Prompt Blocks — Ragnar

    This file contains reusable prompt fragments describing the character.

    Each block can be copied into prompts depending on the generation task.

    Character Block

    Use for:

    • reference sheet prompts
    • anatomy prompts
    • body anchors
    • general character prompts

    Ragnar is an extremely tall warrior (208 cm / 6'10") with a massive, dense muscular build and a broad shoulder-dominant frame. His physique is defined by thick shoulders, a powerful chest, and heavily muscled arms, creating a silhouette that reads as physically overwhelming and upper-body dominant. He has a square facial structure, a sharp jawline, calm blue eyes, a short blond beard, and long shoulder-length blond hair with natural volume. His overall aesthetic combines rugged utilitarian armor with military and gothic influences, emphasizing durability, leather, antique metal, and battle-worn materials.

    Identity Block (Short)

    Use for:

    • quick prompts
    • scene prompts
    • interaction prompts

    Ragnar is an extremely tall, massively muscular warrior with broad shoulders, a square jaw, calm blue eyes, long blond hair, and a short beard. His presence is imposing and grounded, with a rugged utilitarian warrior aesthetic.

    Identity Block (Extended)

    Use for:

    • character setup
    • new chat initialization
    • style-sensitive prompts

    Ragnar is an extremely tall man (208 cm / 6'10") with a massive, dense muscular build and a strongly shoulder-dominant frame. His physique is defined by extremely broad shoulders, a thick powerful torso, and muscular arms that create a heavy upper-body silhouette. His posture is grounded and stable, giving him a calm but physically dominant presence.

    His face has a square shape with a sharp jawline and strong masculine bone structure. He has calm blue eyes with a steady, watchful gaze. His hair is long, blond, and naturally wavy, falling to his shoulders with loose volume. A short blond beard and mustache frame his face and reinforce his rugged warrior appearance.

    Ragnar’s overall aesthetic blends rugged utilitarian armor with military and gothic influences. His clothing and equipment emphasize durability and battle-worn function, using materials like leather and aged metal in dark, practical colors.

    Face Block

    Use for:

    • face anchor prompts
    • portrait prompts
    • expression prompts

    Ragnar has a square facial structure with a strong, angular bone structure and a sharp jawline. His eyes are calm blue with a steady, watchful focus. His hair is long blond hair with natural volume, usually worn loose to shoulder length. He has a short blond beard and mustache that give him a rugged masculine appearance.

    Body Block

    Use for:

    • anatomy prompts
    • pose prompts
    • body anchor prompts

    Ragnar is extremely tall (208 cm / 6'10") with a massive muscular build and a strongly shoulder-dominant frame. His shoulders are extremely broad, his chest is thick and powerful, and his arms are heavily muscled. His torso is dense and solid, while his legs are strong and athletic but visually secondary to the heavy upper-body mass. His posture is grounded and stable, with a powerful stance that emphasizes physical weight and balance.

    Style Block

    Use for:

    • outfit prompts
    • design language sheets
    • wardrobe prompts

    Ragnar’s aesthetic is rugged utilitarian with military and gothic influences. His clothing emphasizes durability, protection, and practicality rather than decorative fashion. Common materials include leather and aged antique metal. His palette centers on dark practical colors such as black and dark brown, with occasional accents of deep red or steel. His equipment often includes armored gauntlets, belts, and battle-worn gear.

    Movement Block

    Use for:

    • motion prompts
    • pose prompts
    • dynamic scenes

    Ragnar moves with grounded, powerful control. His movements are deliberate and efficient rather than quick or agile. His posture remains stable and balanced, and his physical presence fills space naturally. Even small movements feel heavy and purposeful.

    Expression Block

    Use for:

    • expression sheets
    • portrait prompts
    • emotional scenes

    Ragnar’s default expression is serious and controlled, with a calm, focused gaze. His emotional presentation is restrained and composed. When he smiles, it is rare but broad and genuine, briefly softening his otherwise stoic demeanor.

    Wardrobe Description Block

    Use for:

    • outfit sheets
    • design language sheets
    • wardrobe prompts

    Ragnar typically wears rugged utilitarian clothing or armor designed for durability and combat readiness. His outfits often include layered leather garments, reinforced armor pieces, and heavy cloaks. Silhouettes emphasize his broad shoulders and massive torso. His clothing combinations often feature armored gauntlets, belts, long cloaks, and battle-worn equipment. Materials are primarily leather and aged metal, with dark colors dominating the palette.

    Anti-Drift Rules

    Use for:

    • identity preservation prompts
    • difficult scenes
    • regeneration corrections

    Always preserve:

    • extremely tall imposing height
    • massive muscular upper-body dominance
    • broad shoulders and thick chest structure
    • square facial structure
    • long blond shoulder-length hair
    • short blond beard
    • rugged warrior aesthetic

    Avoid:

    • making him lean or lightly athletic
    • reducing shoulder width or upper-body mass
    • shortening his hair significantly
    • turning armor into ornate decorative fantasy costume
