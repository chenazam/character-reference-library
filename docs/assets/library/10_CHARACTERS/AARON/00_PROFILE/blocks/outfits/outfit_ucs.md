## Aaron — Outfit (UCS)

Outfit intent:

This outfit is a refined, realistic interpretation of Aaron’s original design, maintaining its color identity and visual energy while translating it into a wearable, non-costume form.

The goal is to reinforce Aaron’s light, agile, and slightly expressive presence without introducing heaviness or artificial stylization.

Clothing description:

- A sleeveless athletic tank top with a clean, modern cut.
- Base color is deep black.

- Subtle orange design accents are integrated into the garment:
  - Use minimal geometric paneling or seam lines rather than bold graphic shapes.
  - Orange elements should be clean, restrained, and integrated into the structure of the garment, not printed as large graphics.

- The tank top follows the body lightly:
  - Close enough to show natural torso shape
  - Loose enough to avoid emphasizing muscle definition or creating a “trained” look

- Slim-fit athletic pants in a muted orange tone.
- Pants are straight or slightly tapered, with a clean silhouette and minimal detailing.

- Footwear (optional): simple low-profile shoes in neutral or black/orange tones.

Design constraints:

- Avoid bold, high-contrast graphic designs.
- Avoid sharp, angular anime-style paneling.
- Avoid costume-like or overly stylized elements.
- Keep all design details subtle and believable.

Silhouette behavior:

- Clothing must preserve Aaron’s light, lean proportions.
- The tank top must not exaggerate shoulder width or muscle definition.
- The overall silhouette should remain narrow, agile, and relaxed.

Overall impression:

The outfit should feel clean, modern, and slightly expressive, with a hint of stylization through color blocking — but still fully grounded in realistic clothing design.

Avoid any sense of cosplay or exaggerated fashion.
