## Style — Aaron

Style intent:

Aaron’s style is light, expressive, and slightly more intentional than Milo’s, while still remaining natural and wearable.

His clothing reflects a sense of aesthetic awareness without becoming fashion-heavy or artificial.

Core characteristics:

- Clean, modern silhouettes with subtle styling.
- Slightly more refined or intentional than purely functional clothing.
- Visual interest is created through color and design, not complexity.

Color palette:

- Stronger use of contrast and saturation than Milo.
- Key colors include:
  - black (base)
  - orange (accent)
  - additional colors may be introduced if they remain harmonious and not excessive

- Colors may be brighter or more expressive, but should not become chaotic.

Design language:

- Subtle geometric elements, paneling, or color blocking are allowed.
- Patterns should remain minimal and structured.
- Inspiration from natural color systems (e.g. butterflies) can influence color combinations and accents.

Silhouette behavior:

- Clothing should maintain Aaron’s lean, narrow, and agile proportions.
- Avoid adding visual weight or bulk.
- Shapes should feel light and slightly elongated.

Styling constraints:

- Avoid overly complex or layered fashion looks.
- Avoid costume-like or highly stylized designs.
- Avoid heavy textures or materials.

Overall impression:

Aaron’s style should feel fresh, light, and slightly expressive — visually interesting without ever appearing forced or overly designed.
