## Wardrobe — Aaron

Wardrobe intent:

Aaron’s wardrobe consists of clean, modern, lightly styled clothing that emphasizes agility, lightness, and subtle visual interest.

Core pieces:

- Sleeveless tops or lightweight shirts
- Slim-fit or straight-fit pants
- Light, flexible footwear

Design characteristics:

- Use of subtle color blocking or paneling
- Controlled use of accent colors (especially orange)
- Clean construction with minimal detailing

Material behavior:

- Lightweight fabrics
- Smooth, clean surfaces
- Minimal texture noise

Constraints:

- Clothing must not exaggerate muscle definition or shoulder width.
- Avoid heavy layering or bulky garments.
- Avoid loud or overly graphic designs.

Optional variation:

- Occasional use of slightly more expressive color combinations inspired by natural patterns (e.g. insects), but always restrained and harmonious.

Overall impression:

A clean, agile, and slightly expressive wardrobe that feels intentional but effortless.
