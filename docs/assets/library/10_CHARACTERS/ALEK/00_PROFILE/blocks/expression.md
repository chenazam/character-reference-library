Expression:

Default:

- composed expression with subtle underlying intent
- gaze feels engaged, observant, and slightly knowing
- emotional presentation is controlled but not emotionally distant

Smile:

- subtle, often asymmetrical smirk rather than a fully neutral expression
- may carry a hint of amusement, challenge, or quiet confidence
- appears naturally rather than feeling restrained or withheld

Emotional tone:

- confident
- magnetic
- slightly dangerous
- attentive and engaged rather than guarded
- carries a sense of curiosity or quiet challenge

Duality:

- public-facing presence is composed and self-assured, but not emotionally closed
- intensity is present even at baseline through eye engagement and subtle expression shifts
- stronger intensity manifests through increased focus and directness rather than emotional restraint

Behavioral tendencies:

- gaze often fixes fully on the other person rather than drifting outward
- expression may suggest intent, interest, or quiet invitation
- emotional presence feels active rather than passive or withheld

Avoid:

- blank or purely neutral expressions with no underlying intent
- overly soft, passive, or emotionally distant presentation
- exaggerated, playful, or performative flirtation
