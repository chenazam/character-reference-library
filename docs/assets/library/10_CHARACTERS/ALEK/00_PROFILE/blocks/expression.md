Expression:

Default:

- confident neutral expression
- controlled and composed facial presentation
- emotional restraint with subtle underlying intensity

Smile:

- subtle, restrained smile
- appears selectively and with intent

Emotional tone:

- controlled
- confident
- slightly guarded
- quietly intense
- composed rather than openly expressive

Duality:

- public-facing presence is polished, restrained, and self-controlled
- intensity should feel contained beneath the surface rather than openly displayed
- stronger emotional or physical intensity should only emerge in explicitly appropriate contexts

Avoid exaggerated, overly playful, or overtly flirtatious expressions unless explicitly requested.
