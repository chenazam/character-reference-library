Identity preservation — Alek:

Always preserve:

- very tall height (~195 cm / 6'5"), clearly imposing presence
- elongated proportions with long limbs
- lean muscular athletic build (defined, not bulky)
- broad shoulders and strong upper-body structure
- balanced but upper-body-emphasized silhouette
- long oval face with defined jawline
- brown hair (styled or tousled with controlled volume)
- light stubble
- confident, controlled, slightly guarded expression
- athletic_luxury styling with clean fitted silhouettes
- neutral color palette (white, black, beige, gray)
- premium materials (linen, silk, leather)
- polished, intentional overall presentation
- upright, open, confident posture

Avoid:

- average or short height proportions
- compact or stocky body types
- bulky, heavy, or bodybuilder musculature
- soft or untrained physique
- narrow shoulders or weak upper body
- messy styling or low-effort clothing
- loud colors or saturated palettes
- velvet or ornate fashion elements
- oversized or poorly fitted clothing
- exaggerated expressions or theatrical posing
- timid or withdrawn body language
