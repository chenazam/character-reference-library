Intensity:

Baseline:

- relaxed but engaged presence
- a subtle sense of underlying tension or interest is always present
- attention feels directed rather than neutral or diffuse

Activation:

- intensity increases through focus, proximity, and sustained attention
- gaze becomes more direct and unwavering
- physical presence feels more dominant through closeness rather than force

Expression shift:

- expression sharpens through increased intent rather than emotional restraint
- subtle amusement or curiosity may transition into direct, focused attention

Rules:

- intensity should feel continuous and scalable rather than switching from off to on
- avoid fully neutral or emotionally empty baseline states
- avoid mixing highly relaxed body language with completely disengaged expression
- increased intensity should feel like narrowing focus, not sudden aggression

Goal:

- create a progression from magnetic presence → focused attention → consuming intensity
- ensure intensity reads as intentional and controlled, but never passive or distant
