Intensity:

Baseline:

- controlled, composed, and restrained
- intensity is present but contained beneath the surface

Activation:

- intensity increases through posture, gaze, and physical tension
- expression becomes sharper, more focused, and more direct

Rules:

- intensity should scale with context, not appear at full strength by default
- avoid mixing relaxed casual presentation with high-intensity expression
- avoid constant high-intensity output across all assets

Goal:

- create a clear contrast between controlled presence and activated intensity
