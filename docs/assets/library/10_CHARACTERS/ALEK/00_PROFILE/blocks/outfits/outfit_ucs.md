# UCS Outfit — Alek

## Core concept

Alek’s UCS outfit expresses controlled presence with a subtle edge of unpredictability.

The look is refined and intentional, but never sterile.  
There is always a slight sense of tension — as if the styling is composed, but not overly restrained.

---

## Silhouette priorities

- reinforces tall, elongated proportions (6'5" read must remain intact)
- strong shoulder line with relaxed structure
- clean taper through the waist
- long, uninterrupted leg line
- overall silhouette should feel vertical, fluid, and confident

---

## Outfit composition

### Top

- fitted or slightly relaxed t-shirt or lightweight top
- neckline slightly open or relaxed (not tight crew-only look)
- fabric should drape naturally over the torso without clinging excessively

Color options:

- black
- off-white
- soft neutral tones

---

### Outerwear (signature element)

- structured leather jacket (primary identity anchor)
- clean design with minimal hardware
- strong shoulder presence without stiffness
- worn open to preserve vertical line and reveal underlying layer

Color:

- black or deep charcoal

---

### Bottom

- tailored trousers or clean-cut fitted pants
- mid-to-high rise to reinforce leg length
- no excess bunching or break at the ankle

Color:

- black
- charcoal
- deep neutral tones

---

### Footwear

- minimal leather sneakers or sleek boots
- understated, high-quality, no visual noise

Color:

- black or dark neutral

---

## Material direction

- leather (key material)
- soft structured cotton
- linen or lightweight woven fabric

materials should feel tactile and real, not decorative

---

## Styling details

- slight relaxation in styling (e.g. sleeves subtly pushed, collar slightly open)
- nothing should feel overly arranged or symmetrical
- allow minor asymmetry to enhance realism and presence
- outfit should feel worn with intention, not styled for display

---

## Expression alignment

- posture relaxed but grounded
- presence confident, physically aware
- styling should support a “magnetic but dangerous” read rather than purely composed

---

## Avoid

- overly rigid or perfectly symmetrical styling
- oversized or baggy silhouettes
- loud colors or strong contrast
- excessive layering
- sterile or overly minimal “catalog” presentation
