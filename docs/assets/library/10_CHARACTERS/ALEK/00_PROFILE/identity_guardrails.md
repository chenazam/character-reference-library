# Identity Guardrails — Alek

These rules define traits that must remain consistent when generating images of Alek.

## Core Physical Identity

Always preserve:

- very tall height (~195 cm / 6'5") with clearly imposing presence
- elongated frame with long limbs and extended proportions
- lean muscular athletic build with low body fat
- broad shoulders and strong upper-body structure
- defined but not bulky musculature
- balanced but upper-body-emphasized silhouette
- overall silhouette reads as tall, broad, and imposing

Avoid:

- short or average height proportions
- compact or condensed body proportions
- heavy, bulky, or powerlifter-style musculature
- soft or untrained body composition
- narrow shoulders or weak upper-body structure
- excessive mass that removes the streamlined athletic read

---

## Facial Identity

Always preserve:

- long oval face shape
- defined jawline with structured but not exaggerated angularity
- brown hair with natural volume (styled or slightly tousled)
- light stubble (clean, controlled facial hair)
- strong brow and balanced facial proportions
- confident, controlled, slightly guarded gaze

Avoid:

- round or overly soft facial structure
- overly sharp or exaggerated angular stylization
- clean-shaven or heavy beard extremes
- messy, unstructured, or overly long hairstyles
- aging shifts (too young or too aged)
- overly expressive or exaggerated facial emotion

---

## Style Identity

Always preserve:

- athletic_luxury aesthetic as the dominant style
- clean, fitted clothing with refined silhouettes
- high-quality materials (linen, silk, leather)
- neutral color palette (white, black, beige, gray)
- polished, intentional styling even in casual looks

Avoid:

- velvet or ornate luxury materials
- overly casual, sloppy, or low-effort outfits
- loud colors or saturated palettes
- oversized or poorly fitted clothing
- heavily layered or visually noisy styling
- costume-like or theatrical fashion

---

## Body Language

Typical movement:

- deliberate, controlled, and intentional movement
- smooth but confident physical presence

Typical posture:

- upright, open, and confident stance
- shoulders set, chest open, relaxed but dominant posture

Avoid:

- timid, withdrawn, or closed-off posture
- nervous, restless, or overly energetic movement
- exaggerated posing or theatrical gestures
- passive or submissive body language
