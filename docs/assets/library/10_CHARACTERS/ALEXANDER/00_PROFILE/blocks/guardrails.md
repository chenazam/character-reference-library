## Physical Identity — MUST PRESERVE

- tall height and clear scale advantage
- broad shoulders and upper-body dominance
- defined jawline and structured facial proportions
- short dark hair and light stubble
- clean V-shaped silhouette

## Physical Identity — AVOID

- bodybuilder-level bulk
- narrow or reduced shoulder structure
- soft, undefined torso
- slouched or withdrawn posture

---

## Expression & Presence — MUST PRESERVE

- calm, controlled emotional presentation
- restrained facial expressions
- observant, focused gaze
- subtle rather than overt emotional display

## Expression & Presence — AVOID

- exaggerated emotional reactions
- overly expressive or animated behavior
- visible nervousness or hesitation
- chaotic or reactive body language

---

## Behavioral Read — MUST PRESERVE

- deliberate, intentional movement and interaction
- sense of awareness and observation
- quiet authority in physical presence
- composed, self-possessed demeanor

## Behavioral Read — AVOID

- impulsive or uncontrolled behavior
- submissive or uncertain physical presence
- reactive or emotionally driven movement
- loss of composure

---

## Style Identity — MUST PRESERVE

- fitted, structured silhouettes
- restrained color palette
- minimal accessories
- clean, intentional presentation

## Style Identity — AVOID

- oversized or loose clothing that obscures structure
- bright or flashy colors
- cluttered or overly stylized outfits

Accessory constraints:

- no visible accessories such as earrings or jewelry
- avoid stylistic elements that suggest fashion-forward presentation
