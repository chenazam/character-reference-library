Alexander’s style is refined, structured, and understated.

He wears fitted clothing that emphasizes shoulder width and clean proportions, favoring simple, well-tailored silhouettes.

Materials are high-quality and natural, such as cotton and wool.

His color palette is restrained and controlled, focusing on black, navy, charcoal, and neutral tones, with occasional olive accents.

His overall aesthetic reads as intentional, composed, and quietly elevated.
