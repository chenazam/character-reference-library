Expression:

Default:

- calm neutral expression with relaxed confidence

Smile:

- controlled, confident smile that appears deliberate rather than spontaneous

Emotional tone:

- composed
- self-assured
- dominant
- socially confident
- subtly flirtatious without playfulness
- baseline expression must never read as passive or blank
- even in neutral states, maintain subtle facial tension and intentional presence
- eyes should feel focused and aware, not unfocused or idle
- mouth should carry slight natural tension, avoiding overly relaxed softness

Avoid exaggerated or overly expressive facial behavior.

All expressions must remain controlled, deliberate, and restrained.

Avoid:

- overly friendly or socially open expressions
- exaggerated or symmetrical smiles
- playful, boyish, or carefree emotional reads

Expressions should feel:

- intentional rather than reactive
- slightly guarded even when positive
- grounded in confidence and control

Smiles must:

- be subtle or partial
- avoid full toothy grins unless explicitly specified
- maintain tension in the mouth and eyes

Variation requirement:

- expressions should remain controlled and subtle, but still clearly distinguishable from one another
- differences should be expressed through small but readable changes in mouth tension, eye focus, and brow position
- avoid expressions collapsing into identical neutral reads

Baseline expression refinement:

- introduce slight natural asymmetry in the mouth at rest
- one side of the mouth may sit subtly higher than the other
- avoid perfectly centered or mirrored lip positioning

- expression should carry a faint underlying smirk or knowing confidence
- avoid fully neutral or emotionally blank baseline

Neutral expression is NOT soft or passive.

- baseline expression carries subtle confidence and control
- eyes are slightly narrowed or relaxed, never wide or alert
- eyelids sit slightly heavy, giving a grounded, self-assured look
- mouth rests with faint asymmetry or micro-tension, never perfectly balanced
- expression should feel like he is aware of his presence and not trying to appear approachable

Expression behavior:

- baseline expression is controlled and slightly guarded
- eyes carry a subtle evaluating or appraising quality rather than openness
- expression is not openly warm or inviting
- emotional signals are restrained and minimal rather than expressive
- the face should feel composed and internally regulated rather than reactive

Mouth behavior:

- maintain slight natural asymmetry in the mouth
- often reads as a restrained or implied smirk rather than a full smile
- avoid perfectly symmetrical or neutralized mouth shapes
- avoid soft, rounded, or overly friendly resting expressions

Eye behavior:

- gaze should feel steady and assessing rather than engaging
- avoid wide, bright, or openly expressive eyes
- avoid warmth that reads as immediate friendliness
- maintain a subtle sense of distance or evaluation in the gaze
