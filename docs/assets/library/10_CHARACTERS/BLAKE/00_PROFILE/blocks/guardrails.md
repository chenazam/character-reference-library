Identity preservation:

Always preserve:

- height around 6'3"
- tall, broad, upper-body-dominant physique
- wide shoulders and large chest
- thick muscular arms
- stable athletic waist (not narrow)
- balanced but secondary lower body
- strong, grounded silhouette
- short dark brown hair
- masculine, structured facial features
- controlled, high-status clothing style
- upper-body-dominant mass distribution

Avoid:

- compact or short body proportions
- leg-dominant or lower-body-heavy builds
- narrow shoulders or reduced upper-body mass
- exaggerated leanness or lightweight appearance
- overly expressive or playful demeanor
- bright or flamboyant styling
- loose or oversized clothing
- evenly distributed muscular definition
- soft or delicate silhouette
