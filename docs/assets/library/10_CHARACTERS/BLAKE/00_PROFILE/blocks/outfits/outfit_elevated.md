Outfit: elevated fitted casual

Core concept:

- clean, high-status presentation
- tailored fit emphasizing upper-body structure
- refined but not formal
- masculine, controlled, and confident without flashiness

Top:

- fitted long-sleeve dress shirt
- lightweight cotton or stretch blend
- sleeves may be:
  - fully down and clean, OR
  - casually rolled to mid-forearm (controlled, not messy)
- top 1–2 buttons open (no tie)
- shirt is tucked in cleanly

Fit behavior:

- strong fit across chest and shoulders
- slight taper through waist
- no excess fabric or looseness
- fabric should subtly follow the body without appearing tight or stretched

Colors (top):

- deep navy (primary option)
- charcoal
- muted slate tones
- white

Avoid:

- loud patterns
- high contrast prints
- overly glossy fabrics

---

Bottom:

- tailored trousers or high-end slim chinos
- structured but not stiff
- clean taper from thigh to ankle
- slight break or very minimal break at the shoe

Fit behavior:

- follows leg shape without clinging
- no bagginess or excess stacking
- silhouette should feel intentional and controlled

Colors (bottom):

- charcoal
- dark navy
- mid-gray
- black (secondary option)

Avoid:

- overly casual materials
- jogger cuffs
- distressed or textured fabrics

---

Footwear:

- dark leather loafers (black or deep brown)
- clean minimal design, no heavy ornamentation
- sleek silhouette, slightly refined shape

Avoid:

- chunky soles
- athletic trainers
- overly formal dress shoes

Footwear refinement:

- dark leather loafers paired with dark socks matching the trousers
- no visible ankle; maintain a continuous clean line from trouser to shoe

---

Accessories:

- metal watch (silver or steel)
- optional subtle chain (low visibility)
- no excessive jewelry

---

Silhouette priority:

- strong shoulder line
- clean chest structure
- visible waist taper
- overall silhouette reads tall, structured, and controlled

---

Vibe:

- composed, confident, and high-status
- reads as someone who naturally occupies space
- no need to show off — presence does the work

---

Avoid:

- flamboyant styling
- soft or delicate fashion cues
- overly relaxed or oversized fits
- “office worker” stiffness
- fashion-forward experimentation

---

Notes:

- this is not business formal
- this is not casual wear
- this sits in the space of:
  → upscale casual
  → nightlife-ready without trying
  → “effortlessly put together”
