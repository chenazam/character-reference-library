Outfit: bold summer luxury

Core concept:

- confident, attention-grabbing presentation
- relaxed but intentional styling
- masculine, bold, and slightly provocative without losing control
- reads as socially dominant and self-assured

---

Top:

- lightweight short-sleeve button-up shirt
- worn open or partially unbuttoned
- breathable fabric (linen, viscose, or lightweight cotton)

Material quality:

- fabric should read slightly elevated and premium
- very subtle natural sheen may be present under light (not glossy)
- material should appear smooth, fluid, and well-constructed
- avoid high reflectivity or silk-like shine

Fit behavior:

- tailored through shoulders and chest
- slightly relaxed through lower torso for movement, while maintaining a closer fit through chest and upper torso
- subtle taper toward the waist to reinforce upper-body dominance without appearing tight
- not oversized or baggy

Patterns / colors:

- bold but controlled patterns:
  - leopard print (primary reference)
  - dark floral
  - tonal geometric patterns

Color palette:

- warm neutrals (tan, brown, cream)
- black-based patterns
- muted luxury tones

Avoid:

- bright neon colors
- overly playful or cartoonish prints

---

Layering:

- shirt may be worn:
  - open over bare chest (preferred)
  - OR lightly unbuttoned (2–3 buttons open)

---

Bottom:

Option A (preferred):

- tailored swim shorts or short summer trousers
- mid-thigh length
- clean, fitted silhouette

Option B:

- lightweight tailored summer pants
- slightly relaxed but still tapered

Fit behavior:

- emphasizes thighs and glutes subtly
- no bagginess or excess length

Colors:

- black (primary)
- deep brown
- muted olive

---

Footwear:

- minimal slides OR leather sandals (casual luxury)
- OR clean low-profile sneakers (secondary option)

Avoid:

- bulky footwear
- formal shoes

---

Accessories:

- chain necklace (clearly visible, slightly more present, still masculine and controlled)
- should sit naturally against the chest without drawing primary focus
- metal watch
- optional bracelet

---

Silhouette priority:

- strong upper-body emphasis
- visible chest exposure
- clean waist-to-hip taper
- legs read athletic and confident

---

Vibe:

- relaxed dominance
- socially confident, slightly provocative
- “I know I look good and I’m enjoying it”

---

Avoid:

- soft, delicate, or feminine presentation
- overly flamboyant styling
- exaggerated posing or performance energy
- costume-like fashion

---

Notes:

- this is Blake in a social / leisure setting
- confidence is expressed through ease, not effort
- body is visible, but not posed or flexed for attention
