Outfit: signature

Concept:

- masculine, body-aware casual outfit with an athletic edge
- effortless, confident presentation centered on physique rather than styling
- relaxed but intentional, with a natural emphasis on upper-body structure

Top:

- fitted short-sleeve henley shirt
- soft, structured fabric that follows the body without appearing tight or restrictive
- 2–3 button placket, worn slightly open at the collar
- sleeves end mid-bicep and lightly contour the arms
- fabric clearly defines chest, shoulders, and upper torso without exaggeration
- solid colors or very subtle texture only
- colors: neutral or muted tones (black, charcoal, navy, deep olive, off-white)

Bottom:

- fitted athletic joggers with a clean tapered silhouette
- slight ankle crop or cuff to maintain a sharp lower-leg line
- fabric should follow the leg shape without excess looseness
- avoid straight-leg or chino-like structure
- streamlined silhouette with slight taper toward the ankle
- mid-rise fit, sitting naturally at the waist
- no belt; waistband should appear clean and minimal
- fabric may have slight stretch but must retain structure and shape
- neutral tones (black, charcoal, gray, sand, or muted earth tones)

Footwear:

- clean low-profile sneakers
- simple, masculine design with minimal detailing
- neutral color palette (white, black, or tonal variants)

Accessories (minimal and intentional):

- optional watch with a clean, masculine design
- optional subtle chain necklace, partially visible at the open collar

Fit and silhouette priorities:

- shoulders and chest clearly defined through the shirt
- arms read as thick and structured through sleeve fit
- waist remains secondary, maintaining a natural V-shaped silhouette
- lower body supports the upper-body dominance without drawing focus
- overall silhouette reads athletic, grounded, and physically confident
- fabric sits close to the body with light tension across chest and shoulders
- avoid loose drape or relaxed fit through the torso

Styling behavior:

- outfit should feel natural and unforced, as if worn without deliberate styling effort
- clothing supports the body rather than drawing attention to itself
- overall presentation should read confident and socially dominant without appearing performative

Avoid:

- button-up shirts or overly styled tops
- oversized or loose silhouettes
- skinny or delicate styling
- overly technical or gym-specific athletic wear
- loud branding, patterns, or bright colors
- excessive accessories or decorative elements
- anything that reads as fashion-forward, curated, or trying too hard
