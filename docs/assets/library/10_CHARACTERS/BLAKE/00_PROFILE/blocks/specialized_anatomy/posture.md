Posture:

- upright, grounded posture
- shoulders naturally broad and level
- chest slightly forward without exaggeration
- spine straight with minimal curvature
- stance is stable and evenly balanced

Weight distribution:

- evenly distributed or slightly back-weighted
- feet planted firmly
- no light or shifting stance

Upper body behavior:

- shoulders remain open and relaxed
- chest remains visually dominant in all positions
- minimal collapse or inward rotation

Presence:

- occupies space naturally without effort
- stillness conveys confidence rather than stiffness
- posture reads as controlled and physically assured

Avoid:

- slouched or relaxed-collapse posture
- narrow or inward shoulder positioning
- forward head tilt or submissive stance
- dynamic or overly expressive posing as default
