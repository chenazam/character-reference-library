Specialized anatomy focus: upper body

- broad, dominant shoulders
- large, dense chest mass
- thick, powerful arms
- upper body acts as the primary mass anchor

Shape characteristics:

- strong horizontal width through shoulders
- chest projects forward as a central mass
- arms read as thick and heavy rather than sharply defined
- upper body visually outweighs lower body

Maintain:

- clear upper-body dominance in all views
- natural integration with torso and waist
- muscular but not exaggerated or bodybuilder-like proportions

Avoid:

- reduction of shoulder width or chest mass
- flat or underdefined upper torso
- lower-body dominance overriding upper-body emphasis
- overly sharp or segmented muscle definition
