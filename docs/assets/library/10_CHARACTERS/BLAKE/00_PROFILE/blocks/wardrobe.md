Wardrobe:

Clothing should emphasize:

- broad shoulders and chest
- thick arms and upper-body mass
- tall, dominant frame

Core wardrobe logic:

- clothing reinforces physical dominance and presence
- body is revealed selectively and intentionally
- upper body remains the visual focal point
- presentation should feel effortless, not styled for attention

Common elements:

- fitted t-shirts and athletic tops
- tank tops emphasizing shoulders and arms
- open or partially unbuttoned shirts
- tapered joggers or fitted pants
- short structured athletic shorts
- clean sneakers or minimal footwear

Accessories:

- simple chain necklace (often visible)
- metal watch (status signal)
- sunglasses (casual dominance)

Signature approach:

- fitted black or dark-toned tops emphasizing chest and shoulders
- occasional open shirt exposing chest in relaxed settings

Style tone:

- confident
- dominant
- composed
- socially high-status

Avoid:

- oversized or loose silhouettes
- bright playful styling
- overly styled or fashion-forward looks
- attention-seeking through exaggeration
