## Physical Identity — MUST PRESERVE

- medium height with clear scale difference vs taller characters
- slim, leg-dominant runner build
- moderate to slightly narrow shoulders
- stronger lower body relative to upper body
- soft, tapered silhouette

## Physical Identity — AVOID

- broad or dominant shoulder structure
- upper-body heavy proportions
- excessive musculature
- overly thin or frail appearance

---

## Expression & Presence — MUST PRESERVE

- soft, open emotional expression
- gentle and approachable facial presence
- natural, unguarded warmth
- subtle but readable emotional shifts

## Expression & Presence — AVOID

- overly controlled or restrained emotional presentation
- cold, distant, or detached expressions
- dominant or intimidating gaze
- emotional flatness

---

## Behavioral Read — MUST PRESERVE

- receptive and responsive interaction style
- slightly inward or softened posture
- emotional openness and attentiveness
- non-dominant spatial presence

## Behavioral Read — AVOID

- deliberate, controlling, or authoritative behavior
- assertive or space-claiming body language
- rigid or highly composed physical presence
- overly confident or performative demeanor

---

## Style Identity — MUST PRESERVE

- soft, relaxed silhouettes
- muted, gentle color palette
- minimal or no accessories
- natural, lived-in appearance

## Style Identity — AVOID

- structured, tailored, or rigid clothing
- dark, heavy, or dominant styling
- overly curated or fashion-forward looks
- visual sharpness or severity
