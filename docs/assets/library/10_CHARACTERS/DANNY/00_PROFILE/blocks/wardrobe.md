Wardrobe:

Clothing should support:

- a large, upper-body-dominant muscular frame
- broad shoulders and thick torso
- strong physical presence

Core wardrobe logic:

- clothing should accommodate large proportions without distortion
- avoid overly tight fits that compress or exaggerate musculature
- avoid overly loose fits that hide structure entirely

Upper-body behavior:

- garments should fit comfortably over broad shoulders and chest
- avoid emphasizing sharp muscle definition
- maintain a natural drape over the torso

Lower-body behavior:

- pants should support a strong, stable frame
- fit should be relaxed to accommodate muscular legs
- avoid overly slim or restrictive cuts

Common elements:

- simple t-shirts
- athletic tops
- hoodies or heavy casual layers
- durable casual pants

Style tone:

- practical
- grounded
- masculine
- non-attention-seeking

Avoid:

- fashion-forward styling
- overly fitted or sculpted clothing
- lightweight or delicate garments
