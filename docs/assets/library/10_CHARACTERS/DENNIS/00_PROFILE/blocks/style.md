Dennis’s clothing style is minimal, practical, and understated.

He favors simple, everyday outfits that feel comfortable and unpretentious rather than styled or expressive.

His clothing choices avoid trend-driven fashion, bold statements, or decorative elements.

He prefers clean, functional pieces with a quiet, neutral aesthetic.

Color palette:

- primarily neutral and muted tones
- frequent use of navy as a core color
- grays, desaturated blues, and soft neutrals
- avoids bright, saturated, or high-contrast colors

Silhouette priorities:

- clothing should not emphasize the upper body or midsection
- avoid broadening the shoulders or adding volume to the chest
- avoid tight or clingy fabrics around the abdomen

Fit preferences:

- overall fit is natural and comfortable, not oversized and not tailored
- clothing should feel slightly relaxed without appearing baggy
- structure should remain soft and unobtrusive

Material and presentation:

- matte, non-shiny fabrics
- no heavy textures or visual noise
- no logos, graphics, or branding
- no accessories

Overall impression:

- grounded, casual, and functional
- visually quiet and non-attention-seeking
- clothing should support the character without becoming a focal point
