Wardrobe:

Clothing should support:

- a soft, lower-body-dominant physique
- wider hips and fuller thighs
- a comparatively lighter, less emphasized upper body

Core wardrobe logic:

- clothing should de-emphasize the upper body and midsection
- avoid drawing attention to the chest, shoulders, or stomach
- visual weight should remain neutral or slightly biased toward the lower body
- silhouette should feel grounded and stable, not top-heavy

Fit behavior:

- upper-body garments should be relaxed but not oversized
- avoid tight or clingy fabrics around the torso
- avoid cuts that sharpen or exaggerate shoulder width
- maintain a soft, natural drape over the midsection

Lower-body behavior:

- pants should accommodate hip and thigh volume without distortion
- fabric should fall cleanly from the hips
- avoid excessive tightness, pulling, or compression
- taper should be subtle and not restrict the natural silhouette

Common elements:

- chino-style pants (preferred over denim)
- simple t-shirts or casual tops
- soft, matte fabrics with minimal structure
- neutral, low-contrast footwear

Style tone:

- comfortable
- understated
- practical
- non-attention-seeking

Avoid:

- tight upper-body fits that emphasize chest or stomach
- broadening elements (structured shoulders, heavy layering)
- overly loose or oversized clothing
- strong visual contrast or bold styling
- fashion-forward or attention-grabbing pieces
