Identity preservation:

Always preserve:

- height around 6'2" / 188 cm
- tall, athletic, broad-shouldered frame
- strong upper-body presence with a grounded silhouette
- narrow angular masculine face
- clearly defined jawline and forward chin projection
- strong cheekbone structure
- light blue-green eyes
- short dark brown hair with controlled volume and natural texture
- calm, controlled, observant presence
- quiet authority rather than overt showiness
- athletic-luxury / biker-adjacent masculine styling

Facial structure priorities:

- preserve the narrow angular face shape
- preserve a clear mandibular angle
- preserve visible separation between jaw and neck
- preserve slightly heavy or relaxed eyelids
- preserve subtle natural asymmetry in mouth, brow, and facial planes
- preserve a controlled, slightly guarded baseline expression

Avoid facial drift into:

- rounded or softened jawline
- recessed or weakened chin
- widened or boxy face shape
- overly smooth cheek-to-jaw transition
- bright, open, or eager eye expression
- socially warm or friendly-default neutral
- perfect symmetry
- generic polished “male model” facial balance
- cosmetic beautification or over-refined facial planes
- plastic skin or overly airbrushed rendering

Expression guardrails:

- neutral expression must never read blank or passive
- expression should feel controlled, restrained, and slightly evaluating
- avoid overt friendliness, boyish openness, or softness
- avoid exaggerated intensity that turns into aggression
- emotional changes should remain subtle and internally controlled

Body read:

- tall athletic build with broad shoulders and visible upper-body strength
- grounded, stable, confident posture
- strong chest, shoulders, and arms without bodybuilder exaggeration
- body reads powerful and composed rather than flashy or performance-driven

Avoid body drift into:

- slim or lightweight runner build
- narrow shoulders
- delicate silhouette
- compact or shortened proportions
- exaggerated bodybuilder mass
- soft or submissive body language
- jittery, chaotic, or overexpressive movement

Hair preservation:

- short dark brown hair
- natural density and texture
- controlled styling with lift and shape
- avoid flattening, over-stylizing, or making the haircut too neat and cosmetic

Style preservation:

- fitted masculine silhouettes
- clothing should reinforce chest, shoulders, and upper-body structure
- palette stays grounded and controlled: black, charcoal, white, grey, deep neutrals
- styling reads confident and high-status without looking fashion-performative

Avoid style drift into:

- flamboyant or attention-seeking styling
- oversized or shapeless fits
- overly rugged workwear-heavy styling
- bright playful palettes
- decorative or excessive accessories
- soft, delicate, or androgynous presentation unless explicitly requested

Presence and behavioral read:

- reads as calm, controlled, and dominant in a restrained way
- should feel like someone used to being in control without needing to prove it
- presence is steady, grounded, and self-contained

Avoid:

- social eagerness
- overt softness
- reactive or emotionally noisy demeanor
- any read that makes him feel boyish, naive, or overly approachable

Priority:

- structural facial fidelity takes precedence over attractiveness polish
- controlled presence takes precedence over expressiveness
- realism and identity take precedence over beautification

Critical identity risks:

- defaulting to generic "handsome male model" facial structure
- over-refining jawline into a narrow or tapered shape
- shortening midface proportions
- softening eye depth and intensity
- removing natural asymmetry

Hard constraints:

- jaw must remain wide and structurally grounded
- face must not become narrower or more tapered than reference
- midface length must be preserved
- eyes must not appear wide, bright, or overly open
- expression must not default to friendliness or softness

Failure condition:

- if the face reads as a generic attractive model rather than a distinct individual, the result is incorrect

Hard anti-idealization constraint:

- DO NOT smooth facial planes
- DO NOT refine into model-like symmetry
- DO NOT clean up natural irregularities
- DO NOT beautify structure

Priority override:

- structural identity MUST override perceived attractiveness
