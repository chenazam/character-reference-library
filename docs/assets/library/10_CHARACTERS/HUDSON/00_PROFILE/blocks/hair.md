# HAIR — HUDSON

## Core Description

Short, dark brown hair with a clean, controlled structure. The style is practical and consistent, with minimal variation.

## Shape & Length

- Short on sides, slightly longer on top
- Subtle texture, not overly styled
- Maintains a natural but controlled shape

## Styling Behavior

- Lightly styled, never messy or exaggerated
- Holds shape without appearing stiff
- Slight natural movement, but overall disciplined

## Hairline & Volume

- Defined, masculine hairline
- Moderate volume on top, never fluffy or soft

## Color

- Dark brown, natural tone
- No highlights or artificial variation

## Consistency Rules

- Style remains consistent across contexts
- No dramatic changes in length or shape
- Must always read as controlled and grounded
