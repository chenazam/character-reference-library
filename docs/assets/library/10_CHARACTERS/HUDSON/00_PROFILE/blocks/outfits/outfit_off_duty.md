Outfit Variant A — Grounded / Off-Duty:

Hudson wears a casual, grounded outfit that reflects his natural presence without any performative edge.

Upper body:

- fitted dark charcoal or washed black t-shirt
- slightly relaxed fit (not tight, not loose)
- thicker fabric with subtle natural folds
- maintains shoulder structure and chest readability

Lower body:

- dark denim jeans
- straight fit (not skinny, not baggy)
- matte finish with slight natural wear
- minimal detailing

Footwear:

- simple black boots or minimal sneakers

Style intent:

- effortless, unstyled, and real
- no fashion-forward or editorial feel
- grounded, masculine, and understated

Silhouette priority:

- shoulders and upper body must still lead the visual read
- outfit must not obscure or soften Hudson’s structure
