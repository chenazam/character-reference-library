Outfit Variant B — Sharp / Dangerous:

Hudson wears a refined, high-control version of his signature look with a subtle but unmistakable edge.

Upper body:

- fitted black tank top
- matte fabric, no shine
- clean neckline, thick straps
- maintains strong shoulder and chest definition

Jacket:

- fitted black leather jacket
- structured shoulders with clean lines
- minimal hardware, no excessive zippers or detailing
- slightly cropped length to preserve waist definition
- worn open at all times

Lower body:

- black leather pants
- slim straight fit with slightly more structure than the base version
- matte or low-sheen finish

Footwear:

- black boots with minimal detailing

Style intent:

- controlled, confident, and quietly intimidating
- sharper and more intentional than the base outfit
- no exaggerated biker or fashion elements

Silhouette priority:

- shoulders, chest, and arms must remain dominant
- jacket must enhance structure, not obscure it
