Outfit:

Hudson wears a minimalist, masculine outfit with a subtle biker influence.

Upper body:

- fitted black tank top
- matte fabric with no shine
- snug across chest and shoulders
- thick straps, not narrow
- clean neckline, not deep or stylized
- emphasizes shoulder width and arm definition

Lower body:

- black leather pants
- slim straight fit (not tight, not loose)
- matte or low-sheen leather
- minimal seams and detailing
- structured, clean silhouette

Footwear:

- black boots
- simple, sturdy design
- no excessive detailing

Optional layer:

- fitted black leather jacket
- structured shoulders
- minimal hardware
- worn open

Accessories:

- minimal and restrained
- at most one subtle accessory (e.g. simple ring or chain)

Style intent:

- controlled, confident, and understated
- subtle “bad boy” / biker influence without cliché elements
- clothing must feel natural, not performative or costume-like

Silhouette priority:

- shoulders, chest, and arms must remain the dominant visual read
- outfit must not obscure or soften upper-body structure
