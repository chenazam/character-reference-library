# STYLE — HUDSON

## Core Impression

Grounded, controlled, and physically stable. Hudson’s presence is defined by structure, restraint, and quiet confidence. He feels solid and unmoving compared to more expressive characters.

## Silhouette Behavior

- Strong, stable silhouette
- Broad shoulders, defined upper body
- Vertical, grounded posture
- Clothing reinforces structure rather than flow

## Fit & Structure

- Structured, fitted clothing that holds shape
- Emphasis on chest, shoulders, and arms
- Clean lines, minimal looseness or drape

## Styling Tendencies

- Minimalistic and consistent
- Avoids experimentation or flamboyance
- Functional and intentional rather than expressive

## Color & Material

- Dark, muted tones dominate
- Heavy, structured materials (leather, denim, thick cotton)
- Matte finishes over soft or reflective surfaces

## Presence in Scenes

- Appears physically stable and grounded
- Clothing remains visually consistent regardless of movement
- Acts as a visual anchor for other characters

## Interaction Contrast (with Jonah)

- Receives rather than initiates movement
- Stabilizes shared space
- His stillness enhances Jonah’s motion and expressiveness
