# WARDROBE — HUDSON

## Core Wardrobe Identity

Minimal, structured, and consistent. Hudson’s wardrobe prioritizes clean lines, durability, and understated masculinity. He dresses with intention but without flash.

## Core Pieces

- Fitted t-shirts (dark neutrals: black, charcoal, deep earth tones)
- Structured outerwear, especially leather jackets
- Straight or slim-fit jeans
- Dark trousers or utility-style pants
- Boots or solid, minimal sneakers

## Outerwear Signature

- Leather jacket is a defining piece
- Adds presence, weight, and visual authority
- Often worn open, framing the torso

## Fit Preferences

- Close to the body but not tight
- Emphasizes shoulders, chest, and arms
- Clean, structured silhouette with minimal drape

## Color Palette

- Dark neutrals: black, grey, deep brown, muted tones
- Low contrast, cohesive combinations
- Avoids bright or playful color accents

## Material Language

- Heavier fabrics: leather, denim, structured cotton
- Matte finishes preferred over soft or flowing materials
- Durable, grounded textures

## Styling Behavior

- Consistent and repeatable outfits
- Minimal variation in silhouette
- Practical and functional rather than expressive

## Footwear

- Solid, grounded shoes: boots or minimal sneakers
- Dark tones, low visual noise
