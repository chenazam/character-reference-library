# BODY — HUNTER

## Core Principle

Hunter’s body communicates power through density and control, not exaggeration.

He is clearly strong and physically capable, but does not appear sculpted for display.
His physique reads as functional, grounded, and inevitable.

## Overall Build

- tall with a broad, dominant upper body
- dense musculature with natural proportions
- visible strength without exaggerated definition
- body reads heavy and grounded rather than light or agile

## Muscle Definition

- moderate, natural definition
- visible chest, shoulder, and arm structure
- abdominal definition present but not sharply cut
- avoid extreme separation or “bodybuilder” striations

## Mass Distribution

- upper body dominant (shoulders, chest, upper back)
- torso feels thick and stable
- limbs are strong and proportional, not oversized

## Surface Quality

- natural skin texture
- subtle softness over muscle (not dry or hyper-defined)
- visible body hair (especially chest) contributes to grounded masculinity

## Proportions

- broad shoulders relative to waist
- moderate taper, not extreme V-shape
- legs proportional, not emphasized over upper body

## Posture

- upright but relaxed
- shoulders naturally set, not artificially pulled back
- stance feels stable and rooted

## Movement Read (important for generation)

Even in a neutral pose, the body should imply:

- strength without tension
- readiness without stiffness
- control without effort

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel: elongated, light, soft → Hunter: dense, grounded, powerful
- Noel: elegant proportions → Hunter: structural presence
- Noel: visually expressive → Hunter: visually stable

## Avoid

- extreme leanness or dryness
- exaggerated V-taper
- stylized or “heroic” anatomy proportions
- overly smooth or hairless presentation
