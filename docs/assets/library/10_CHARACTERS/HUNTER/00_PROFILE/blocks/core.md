# CORE — HUNTER

## Core Identity

Hunter embodies control, stability, and quiet dominance.

He does not perform power or masculinity — he naturally carries them.
His presence is grounded, contained, and decisive.

## Personality Expression (Visual)

- calm and controlled
- rarely reactive
- emotionally contained
- observant rather than expressive

## Social Presence

- naturally commands attention without seeking it
- others adjust to him rather than the reverse
- occupies space with ease and certainty

## Energy Profile

- low volatility
- high control
- steady, grounded intensity

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel expresses → Hunter contains
- Noel moves dynamically → Hunter stabilizes
- Noel invites interaction → Hunter defines boundaries

## Visual Priorities

- grounded physical presence
- controlled expression
- minimal, deliberate movement
- natural, non-performative masculinity

## Identity Anchor

Hunter must always read as:

- in control of himself
- physically and socially dominant
- stable, reliable, and grounded
- emotionally contained but aware
