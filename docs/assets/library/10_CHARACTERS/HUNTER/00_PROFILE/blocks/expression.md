# EXPRESSION — HUNTER

## Core Principle

Hunter’s expression is controlled and restrained.

Emotion exists, but is rarely displayed.

## Default Expression

- neutral, composed
- relaxed but unreadable
- slight tension in the eyes

## Emotional Range

- low external variance
- internal emotion does not translate into overt expression

## Smile Behavior

- rare
- subtle
- often asymmetrical or brief

## Eye Behavior

- steady, direct gaze
- assessing rather than reacting
- maintains eye contact comfortably

## Facial Movement

- minimal
- no exaggerated reactions
- micro-expressions over visible shifts

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel expresses emotion openly → Hunter contains it
- Noel’s face is readable → Hunter’s is controlled
- Noel reacts → Hunter observes

## Interaction with Noel

- allows Noel to be expressive without matching intensity
- slight softening only in close/intimate contexts
- emotional shifts are subtle but meaningful

## Overall Read

Hunter should feel like:

- he is always in control of himself
- nothing escapes unintentionally
- his attention is focused and deliberate
