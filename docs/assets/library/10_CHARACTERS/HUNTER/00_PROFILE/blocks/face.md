# FACE — HUNTER

## Core Principle

Hunter’s face reflects control, maturity, and grounded masculinity.

It should feel structured, stable, and subtly intense — never soft or overly expressive.

## Structure

- face shape: square-leaning oval
- strong, defined jawline
- solid chin with clear structure
- balanced but masculine proportions

## Brow & Eyes

- slightly heavy brow
- deep-set eyes
- gaze is steady, focused, and assessing
- eyelids relaxed, not wide or expressive

## Nose

- straight and proportionate
- natural, not stylized or sharp

## Mouth

- neutral resting position
- lips neither overly full nor thin
- minimal movement in default expression

## Skin & Texture

- natural skin texture (not overly smooth or stylized)
- subtle ruggedness
- avoid “perfect” or airbrushed look

## Facial Hair

- can support light stubble or very short beard
- never overly styled or decorative
- reinforces grounded masculinity

## Expression Behavior

- default: neutral, composed
- emotional expression is subtle and controlled
- micro-expressions preferred over visible changes

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel: soft, expressive, readable → Hunter: structured, controlled, contained
- Noel: emotional clarity → Hunter: emotional restraint

## Identity Lock

Hunter’s face must always read as:

- stable and grounded
- quietly dominant
- controlled and self-contained

## Avoid

- soft or delicate facial structure
- overly youthful or boyish features
- exaggerated masculinity (caricatured jaw, etc.)
- expressive or reactive facial behavior
- “model face” perfection without character
