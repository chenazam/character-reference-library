# GUARDRAILS — HUNTER

## Core Identity Lock

Hunter must always read as:

- physically powerful
- grounded and stable
- controlled and self-contained
- naturally dominant without performance

## Body Guardrails

### MUST PRESERVE

- dense, natural musculature
- strong upper body presence (shoulders, chest)
- moderate, realistic definition
- subtle softness over muscle (not dry or shredded)
- visible body hair for grounded masculinity

### MUST AVOID

- bodybuilder aesthetics (over-defined, striated, overly lean)
- exaggerated muscle size or proportions
- hyper-stylized “fitness model” look
- overly smooth, hairless torso
- fragile or lightweight body appearance

## Proportion Guardrails

### MUST PRESERVE

- broad shoulders with moderate taper
- thick, stable torso
- proportional limbs

### MUST AVOID

- extreme V-shape torso
- narrow or delicate frame
- overly long or stylized limbs
- cartoonishly heroic proportions

## Expression & Presence Guardrails

### MUST PRESERVE

- neutral, controlled expression
- minimal emotional leakage
- steady, focused gaze

### MUST AVOID

- expressive or reactive facial behavior
- exaggerated emotion
- playful or soft demeanor (outside Noel interaction)

## Movement Guardrails

### MUST PRESERVE

- deliberate, economical movement
- grounded physical presence
- minimal gestures

### MUST AVOID

- fidgeting or unnecessary motion
- exaggerated posing
- dynamic or overly expressive movement

## Interaction Guardrails (CRITICAL FOR SCENES)

### WITH NOEL

- Hunter remains the stable anchor
- does not mirror Noel’s expressiveness
- subtle softening only in close/intimate contexts
- dominance is implicit, not acted out

## Rendering Guardrails

### MUST PRESERVE

- natural human anatomy
- believable muscle structure
- realistic surface variation

### MUST AVOID

- hyper-polished or plastic skin
- stylized anatomy exaggeration
- artificial symmetry or perfection

## Failure Modes to Watch For

- "Gym model" → too aesthetic, too posed
- "Bodybuilder" → too defined, too dry
- "Generic male base" → loses density and presence
- "Softened Hunter" → drifts toward Noel’s space

If any of these occur, regenerate with emphasis on:
"natural density", "controlled power", "non-performative masculinity"
