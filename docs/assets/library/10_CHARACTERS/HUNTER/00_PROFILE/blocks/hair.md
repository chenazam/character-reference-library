# HAIR — HUNTER

## Core Principle

Hunter’s hair is controlled, natural, and understated.

It should reinforce structure and masculinity without drawing attention.

## Length & Shape

- short to medium length
- slightly longer on top, shorter on sides
- maintains a clean, controlled silhouette

## Texture

- dense, slightly wavy
- natural movement, but not loose or messy
- hair should feel substantial and grounded

## Styling

- minimal styling product
- controlled but not “styled” in a visible way
- no exaggerated volume or fashion-forward shaping

## Hairline

- natural, slightly mature hairline
- avoid overly sharp or artificial edges

## Color

- dark brown
- rich, natural tone
- avoid overly glossy or stylized rendering

## Behavior in Motion

- remains mostly in place
- minimal bounce or exaggerated movement

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel: soft, expressive, stylized → Hunter: controlled, natural, restrained
- Noel’s hair adds personality → Hunter’s hair reinforces structure

## Identity Lock

Hair must always read as:

- controlled
- masculine
- understated
- effortless

## Avoid

- overly styled or trendy cuts
- excessive volume or fluffiness
- soft, delicate, or “pretty” hair presentation
- messy or chaotic appearance
