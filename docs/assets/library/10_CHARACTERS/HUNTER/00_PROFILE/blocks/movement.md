# MOVEMENT — HUNTER

## Core Principle

Hunter moves with deliberate efficiency.

No movement is wasted.
No gesture is accidental.

## Movement Style

- slow to moderate pace
- deliberate and controlled
- physically grounded

## Weight & Presence

- steps feel heavy and stable
- center of gravity remains low and controlled
- body reads as anchored in space

## Gesture Behavior

- minimal gestures
- precise, intentional movements
- no fidgeting or unnecessary motion

## Interaction with Space

- occupies space naturally
- does not adjust to others — others adjust to him
- movement defines the environment rather than reacting to it

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel moves fluidly → Hunter moves decisively
- Noel fills space dynamically → Hunter stabilizes space
- Noel reacts → Hunter dictates

## Interaction with Noel

- allows Noel’s movement to play around him
- remains stable while Noel is expressive
- creates a visual anchor point in shared scenes

## Overall Read

Hunter should always feel like:

- he knows exactly what he is doing
- he never hesitates
- he never rushes
