Outfit:

- minimal, high-quality modern clothing
- clean, structured silhouette with no exaggeration
- fitted but not tight, following natural body lines
- no layering complexity or unnecessary elements

Top:

- fine knit shirt or minimal button shirt
- clean, structured appearance with subtle tailoring
- smooth, high-quality fabric with soft, controlled drape
- collar (if present) must be minimal and well-formed
- no logos, prints, or decorative elements

Bottom:

- tailored trousers with a clean, straight fit
- structured but comfortable, no looseness or bagginess
- minimal seam visibility and no decorative details

Footwear:

- minimal, clean shoes (low-profile sneakers or equivalent)
- no bold branding or high-contrast elements
- consistent with overall understated aesthetic

Color palette:

- dark neutrals (black, charcoal, deep navy)
- low contrast between pieces
- no bright colors or accent tones

Material behavior:

- matte or softly textured finishes
- no shine, gloss, or reflective surfaces
- materials should read as high-quality and durable

Fit and consistency:

- consistent fit across all views
- clothing must preserve body silhouette and proportions
- no distortion, stretching, or variation between panels

Avoid:

- logos, graphics, or text
- layered or complex outfits
- streetwear styling or fashion-forward elements
- overly casual clothing (e.g. basic t-shirts, hoodies)
- overly formal business attire (e.g. suits, ties)
- visible branding or statement pieces
