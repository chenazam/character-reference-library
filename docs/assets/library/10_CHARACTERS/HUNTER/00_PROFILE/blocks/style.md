# STYLE — HUNTER

## Core Principle

Hunter’s style is controlled, restrained, and intentional.

He does not dress to attract attention.
He dresses to establish presence.

Everything is clean, structured, and quietly expensive.

## Aesthetic Direction

- modern minimal masculine
- quiet luxury
- structured simplicity
- controlled refinement

## Visual Characteristics

- clean silhouettes with strong structure
- minimal layering, but precise fit
- no unnecessary detailing or decoration
- materials carry the visual weight, not design complexity

## Contrast to Noel

- Noel explores expression → Hunter reduces to essentials
- Noel plays with aesthetics → Hunter stabilizes them
- Noel is visually dynamic → Hunter is visually anchored

## Material Behavior

- matte or low-sheen surfaces preferred
- fabrics should feel substantial and grounded
- avoid anything overly soft, flowy, or delicate

## Color Logic

- dark neutrals dominate (black, charcoal, navy)
- occasional controlled contrast (white, muted tones)
- no bright or playful colors

## Overall Read

Hunter should always read as:

- controlled
- grounded
- expensive without trying
- physically and socially dominant
