Face:

- face shape: narrow tapered oval (not wide or boxy)
- jawline: defined but soft, with a smooth taper toward the chin (avoid square or heavy jaw)
- chin: subtle projection, not broad or flat
- cheekbones: moderately defined, not overly pronounced
- eyes: blue, slightly narrow with natural upper eyelid weight (avoid wide or rounded eye shapes)
- brow: natural structure with slight definition, not flat or overly softened
- nose: straight with natural proportion, not overly refined or stylized
- mouth: moderate width with a naturally defined resting shape (avoid overly softened or “pleasant” neutrality)
- skin tone: fair with a natural, slightly outdoor complexion

Facial behavior:

- the face should support clear emotional readability under expression
- structural features should allow warmth and openness when expression is applied
- neutral state must preserve identity without introducing expression bias

Maintain consistent facial proportions and identity.
