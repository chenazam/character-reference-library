Face:

- face shape: balanced oval
- jawline: defined but not overly sharp
- cheekbones: moderately defined
- eyes: expressive blue eyes with natural liveliness and warmth
- skin tone: fair skin with a healthy outdoor tone

Facial behavior:

- the face should read as naturally expressive and emotionally readable
- warmth and amusement should come through clearly when expression is added
- smiles should feel open, genuine, and unguarded rather than restrained or overly subtle
- even in neutral, the face should feel alive and present rather than blank or lifeless

Maintain consistent facial proportions and identity.
