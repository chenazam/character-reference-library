Outfit: Elevated date night (body-aware, attention-focused)

Core intent:

- this outfit must feel deliberately sexy, confident, and attention-aware
- it must read as styled for being looked at, not just “well dressed”
- avoid safe, neutral, or overly preppy presentation

Silhouette:

- short-sleeve button-up shirt worn partially unbuttoned (not fully open)
- no tank layer underneath
- chest must be partially visible
- tailored chino shorts, mid-thigh length
- clean low-profile sneakers or minimal loafers

Fit:

- all garments are fitted and body-aware
- shirt follows the torso without excess looseness
- shorts clearly emphasize thighs and glutes
- waist remains clean and structured (no drawstrings)

Styling behavior:

- shirt is intentionally unbuttoned (top 2–3 buttons open)
- neckline exposure is a key visual element
- sleeves may be slightly rolled or structured to enhance arms
- overall styling must feel intentional, not default
- shirt is tucked into the shorts
- the waistband and belt area must remain visible

Color hierarchy (STRICT):

- shirt = primary visual driver (must be visually interesting)
  - allowed: deep tones (navy, black, dark green), or visible pattern (floral, textured)
- shorts = light warm neutral (ivory, soft stone, warm off-white, or light beige)
- avoid pure bright white unless balanced by strong contrast elsewhere
- outfit must create clear contrast and visual separation

Examples:

- dark navy patterned shirt + tan shorts
- black shirt + off-white shorts
- deep green shirt + warm beige shorts

Avoid:

- plain light beige/white shirt as dominant element
- low-contrast combinations
- desaturated palettes that flatten the look
- outfits that read as safe, preppy, or catalog-basic

Attraction priority:

- the outfit must clearly frame the torso and chest
- visible skin must feel intentional and central to the look
- if the outfit does not read as physically appealing at a glance, it is incorrect

Jasper alignment:

- outfit must support a playful, flirtatious, socially confident presence
- styling should feel like he expects attention and welcomes it
- subtlety is not a priority; controlled boldness is
