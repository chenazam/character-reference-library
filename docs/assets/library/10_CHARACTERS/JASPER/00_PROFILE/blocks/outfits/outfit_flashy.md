outfit_jasper_flashy:

top:
type: lightweight button-up shirt
color: clean white / ivory OR softly tinted pastel (sky blue, soft aqua)
fabric: semi-sheer lace or open-weave material with a clearly visible pattern and transparency
styling: worn fully unbuttoned at all times, no partial closure, exposing chest and upper torso completely
fit: relaxed but controlled drape; follows the torso without clinging or ballooning
behavior: fabric softly reveals skin through pattern and light; transparency is readable but not fragile or see-through in a cheap way

bottom:
type: tailored shorts
color: exactly matching the top (coordinated set)
fabric: same lace or open-weave material; subtly lined for structure while preserving surface texture
fit: slim, clean silhouette with slight contour over hips and glutes
length: mid-thigh, slightly shorter than standard for a more assertive silhouette
rise: mid to slightly high rise to emphasize waist-to-hip transition
behavior: reads as a deliberate set piece; visually striking while remaining controlled and wearable

footwear:
type: refined minimal sandal
color: soft neutral (cream, tan, or white)
styling: low-profile, lightweight, open; no bulk or visual weight
behavior: supports the airy, exposed feel; must not compete with the outfit

accessories:
chain:
type: fine to medium chain necklace
styling: clearly visible against exposed chest; catches light subtly without dominating
watch:
type: minimal, optional
styling: understated; avoid heavy or sporty designs
additional: none

styling_notes:

- the outfit must read as a cohesive matching set, not separate pieces
- transparency and texture are the primary visual statement, not color contrast
- shirt is ALWAYS fully unbuttoned; no mid-chest or partial variations
- skin visibility is intentional, controlled, and integrated into the design
- silhouette remains grounded and clean to avoid costume-like exaggeration
- proportions emphasize hips, waist, and upper thighs subtly but clearly

- overall balance:
  bold in material and exposure, restrained in structure and palette

- overall read:
  striking, seductive, and intentionally attention-grabbing
  confident and unapologetic in presentation
  draws attention immediately without appearing try-hard or theatrical
