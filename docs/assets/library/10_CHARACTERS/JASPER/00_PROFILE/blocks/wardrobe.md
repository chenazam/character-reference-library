Wardrobe:

Clothing should emphasize:

- leg-dominant athletic physique
- strong thighs and prominent glutes
- narrow waist and lighter upper body

Core wardrobe logic:

- short inseam shorts are a defining element
- fit should be snug and body-aware
- clothing should highlight rather than conceal the physique
- upper body is often intentionally revealed or lightly covered

Common elements:

- short fitted shorts (various styles: athletic, casual, yoga, chino)
- fitted tanks, crop tops, or lightweight shirts
- open shirts or partially unbuttoned tops
- casual sneakers or running shoes

Signature piece:

- light pink tank top with a bold black bunny logo on the front

Style tone:

- playful
- flirtatious
- socially confident
- attention-seeking without being exaggerated

Avoid:

- long or baggy shorts
- loose or oversized silhouettes
- heavy layered outfits
- overly formal or conservative styling
