Body:

Jonah has a lean, lightly muscular athletic build with elongated proportions and a strong emphasis on mobility, flexibility, and fluid control.

---

Overall build:

- lean athletic physique with low to moderate body fat
- muscle mass is present but restrained and secondary to movement
- body must never read as bulky, dense, or strength-dominant

- physique optimized for movement, not force
- visual impression: light, elastic, and responsive

---

Overall silhouette:

- tall, elongated silhouette with strong vertical emphasis
- long limbs are a primary visual feature
- torso appears relatively short compared to leg length

- narrow through the torso with a soft, natural taper
- shoulders moderately broad but visually understated
- waist clearly defined but not sharply cinched
- hips natural and relaxed, not narrow or rigid

Silhouette priority:

- legs must visually dominate the silhouette over the torso
- overall read must be vertical and elongated, not compact or grounded

---

Upper body:

- shoulders rounded, mobile, and relaxed
- no squared or rigid shoulder structure
- shoulder width must not dominate the silhouette

- chest lightly defined with soft transitions
- no flat or block-like pectoral structure
- pectoral mass must remain secondary

- arms lean and toned with subtle definition
- no pronounced bicep/tricep separation
- no visual emphasis on arm mass

---

Core:

- strong and stable but visually restrained
- abdominal definition is subtle and continuous
- no deep cuts, no segmented six-pack appearance

- transitions between chest, ribs, and abdomen must remain smooth
- core should read as functional and flexible, not sculpted

---

Lower body:

- long, well-shaped legs are a defining feature
- thighs slightly more developed than upper body but still lean
- leg mass must remain streamlined and not heavy

- calves defined but not bulky

Lower body priority:

- legs must carry visual weight through length, not mass
- avoid thick or power-driven leg appearance

---

Muscle behavior:

- muscles appear soft and responsive at rest
- no hard separation or extreme definition
- no “posed” or flexed appearance

- muscle transitions must be continuous, not segmented
- body must feel capable of immediate movement

---

Body read:

- contemporary dancer / gymnast hybrid
- elastic rather than heavy
- controlled rather than forceful
- expressive rather than restrained

The body must feel:

- light
- fluid
- mobile
- alive

---

Posture:

- upright but relaxed
- shoulders naturally open without tension
- stance must feel effortless and balanced

- subtle natural asymmetry is required (micro weight shift)
- posture must not be perfectly symmetrical or mannequin-like
- body must feel active even at rest

---

Proportion priorities:

- maintain long limb proportions
- avoid shortening or thickening the torso
- preserve a light, elongated vertical flow

---

Mass constraints (critical):

- muscle mass must remain secondary to proportion and movement
- body must not read as dense, compact, or weight-driven

DO NOT:

- increase muscle size significantly
- create heavy chest or shoulder mass
- thicken the torso or neck
- introduce visual density in the frame

Failure condition:

If the body reads as strong, heavy, or power-based → INVALID

---

Silhouette constraints (critical):

- upper body must NOT dominate the silhouette
- shoulders must not outweigh the legs visually
- torso must not become the focal mass

Failure condition:

If attention is drawn to chest/shoulders before legs → INVALID

---

Hudson contrast constraint (critical):

This character must NOT visually overlap with Hudson’s body type.

DO NOT:

- broaden shoulders to dominant width
- increase arm or chest mass
- introduce strong structural definition
- shift balance toward upper-body dominance

Jonah must always read as:

- lighter
- more elongated
- less mass-driven
- more movement-oriented

---

Avoid:

- bulky or heavy musculature
- thick neck or dense upper body
- strong V-taper or pronounced chest plate
- bodybuilder or strength-athlete proportions
- rigid, grounded, or perfectly symmetrical stance
- overly soft or untrained appearance

---

Identity summary:

The body must read immediately as:

- tall and elongated
- lean and athletic
- light and mobile
- expressive and fluid

It must never read as:

- heavy
- dense
- rigid
- strength-dominant
