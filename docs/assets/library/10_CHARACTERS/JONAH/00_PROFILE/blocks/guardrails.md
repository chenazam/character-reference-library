Identity guardrails (strict):

This character must remain visually consistent across all outputs.
All traits below are non-negotiable identity anchors.

Core identity:

- height: tall (178 cm / 5'10")
- build: lean athletic, not bulky
- proportions: elongated, long-limbed
- silhouette: narrow waist, balanced shoulders, vertical emphasis

- face: long oval with refined, balanced features
- eyes: green, expressive
- hair: dark blonde, medium-length, soft wavy, side-swept volume

- overall presence: open, expressive, playful, radiant

Silhouette constraints:

- body must read as tall and elongated at first glance
- limbs must appear long relative to torso
- waist must remain narrow
- shoulders must remain moderate and not dominant

DO NOT:

- increase torso thickness
- shorten limb proportions
- create a stocky or compact silhouette
- shift toward a bulky or power-based build

Failure condition:

If the character reads as a bodybuilder, football player, or compact athletic type → INVALID

---

Facial constraints:

- maintain long oval face shape
- preserve balanced, refined structure
- jawline must be defined but soft, not heavy

- maintain expressive, open eye shape
- preserve green eye color

- hair must remain medium-length, wavy, and side-swept
- hair must contribute to softness and movement

DO NOT:

- square or harden the face
- exaggerate jaw width or heaviness
- shorten hair into cropped or faded styles
- over-style hair into rigid shapes

Failure condition:

If the face reads as rugged, severe, or overly masculine → INVALID

---

Body constraints:

- lean athletic definition only
- visible tone is allowed but must remain light and natural
- no heavy mass accumulation

- torso must remain light and elongated
- muscle definition must not dominate the visual read

DO NOT:

- increase muscle mass significantly
- deepen abs or separation into a "fitness model" look
- broaden shoulders beyond balanced proportions

Failure condition:

If muscle mass becomes a defining feature → INVALID

---

Style constraints:

- aesthetic: playful athletic
- clothing must feel expressive, confident, and slightly flirtatious
- styling should feel natural, not calculated or fashion-forward

Allowed:

- crop tops
- athletic shorts
- mesh fabrics
- expressive sporty outfits

DO NOT:

- default to generic gymwear
- use conservative or muted styling that removes personality
- introduce formal, tactical, or heavy fashion aesthetics

Failure condition:

If the outfit feels generic, muted, or personality-less → INVALID

---

Expression constraints:

- baseline emotional tone: open, warm, expressive
- face should feel alive and responsive

Allowed:

- soft neutral
- warm smile
- playful or curious expressions

DO NOT:

- default to stoic or emotionally closed expressions
- flatten emotional range

Failure condition:

If the character reads as emotionally distant or rigid → INVALID

---

Movement / posture constraints:

- posture must feel relaxed and natural
- body language must feel open and fluid
- stance should reflect ease and confidence

DO NOT:

- use rigid, symmetrical, military-like posture
- create heavy, grounded, aggressive stance

Failure condition:

If the pose reads as stiff, rigid, or overly controlled → INVALID

---

Drift detection priority:

Most common failure modes:

1. body becomes too muscular
2. shoulders become too broad
3. face becomes more rugged / masculine
4. hair becomes shorter or overly clean
5. styling becomes generic athletic wear

If any of the above occur, regeneration is required.

---

Identity priority summary:

Jonah must always read as:

- tall and elongated
- light, athletic, and mobile
- expressive and emotionally open
- soft-featured but still masculine
- playful, radiant, and confident

He must never read as:

- heavy
- rigid
- overly masculine or rugged
- stylistically neutral or muted
