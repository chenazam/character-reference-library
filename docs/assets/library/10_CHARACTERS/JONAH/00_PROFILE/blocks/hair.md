Hair:

Jonah has medium-length dark blonde hair with a soft, natural wave pattern and a relaxed, side-swept flow that contributes strongly to his expressive, approachable appearance.

Overall structure:

- medium length with visible volume and movement
- not short or tightly cropped
- not long enough to reach shoulders
- hair must read as soft, flexible, and naturally styled

Texture:

- soft wavy texture (not straight, not tightly curled)
- loose, natural waves with slight irregularity
- no rigid styling or overly defined curl patterns

Front / fringe behavior:

- front section is longer and naturally falls into a side-swept wave
- soft parting slightly off-center (not a hard part)
- strands gently curve across the forehead without fully covering it
- must frame the face in a relaxed, organic way

Sides:

- sides are shorter than the top but not faded or tightly trimmed
- maintain softness and continuity with the top
- avoid sharp transitions or aggressive undercuts

Back:

- slightly longer than typical short styles
- soft, natural taper
- retains movement and flow rather than being tightly cleaned up

Volume:

- moderate natural volume, especially in the front and top
- lifted but not styled into a fixed shape
- avoid flatness, but also avoid exaggerated height

Color:

- dark blonde
- natural tone with slight warmth
- avoid overly saturated or artificial color shifts
- subtle natural variation is acceptable

Styling behavior:

- appears casually styled or naturally fallen into place
- no visible product-heavy styling (no gelled, spiked, or stiff shapes)
- should feel touchable and soft

Face integration:

- hair must support Jonah’s expressive, open facial read
- enhances softness and approachability rather than sharpness or intensity
- contributes to a youthful, radiant, slightly playful appearance

Avoid:

- short cropped cuts
- buzzcuts or fades
- slicked-back or heavily styled looks
- overly clean or rigid barbered structure
- very long or flowing hair
- overly voluminous “model hair” styling
- flat, lifeless hair with no movement

Consistency priority:

- maintain the same general length, wave pattern, and side-swept structure across all assets
- hair must remain a key identity anchor for Jonah
