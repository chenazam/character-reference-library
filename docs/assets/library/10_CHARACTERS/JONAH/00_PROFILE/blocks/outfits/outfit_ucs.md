Outfit (UCS - default):

- lightweight short-sleeve t-shirt in a soft, natural tone (warm off-white, sun-faded beige, muted sage green, or soft sky blue)
- color should feel slightly warm and lived-in rather than stark or neutral
- fabric appears breathable and lightly draped, not tight or structured
- neckline relaxed and natural

- athletic or casual lightweight shorts, mid-thigh length
- simple and unbranded, with a relaxed fit that allows full freedom of movement

- bare feet or minimal, unobtrusive footwear (if required)

Fit and styling:

- outfit feels effortless and unstyled, as if thrown on without consideration
- slight natural irregularities are allowed (subtle folds, relaxed drape)

- clothing should suggest ease of movement and physical comfort
- silhouette remains light, open, and dynamic

Overall impression:

- simple but full of life
- approachable, warm, and naturally attractive
- never reads as styled or curated, but also never feels generic or lifeless
