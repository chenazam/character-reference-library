Posture:

- relaxed, natural stance with subtle asymmetry
- weight is not perfectly centered; slight shift toward one leg
- hips may be very slightly offset, avoiding a rigid straight alignment

- shoulders are relaxed and not squared
- one shoulder may sit slightly lower than the other
- upper body feels loose and unforced rather than held or posed

- arms hang naturally with a soft bend, not stiff or fully straight
- hands relaxed, with a slight sense of motion or responsiveness

- neck and head posture is upright but not rigid
- head may have a very slight natural tilt, adding to the sense of ease

Overall presence:

- body feels light and fluid rather than grounded or planted
- subtle sense of continuous motion, even while standing still
- posture reflects a dancer’s natural balance, ease, and expressiveness without becoming exaggerated or pose-like
