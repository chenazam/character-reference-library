# STYLE — JONAH

## Core Impression

Soft, expressive, and physically open. Jonah’s style emphasizes movement, lightness, and emotional accessibility. Even when dressed simply, he feels alive and present in his body.

## Silhouette Behavior

- Light, narrow, and flexible silhouette
- Emphasis on waist definition and torso line
- Legs often visually prominent (shorter hems, exposed skin)
- Clothing follows body movement rather than structuring it

## Fit & Structure

- Slim to relaxed fits, never bulky
- Slightly loose or draped fabrics preferred over rigid structure
- Cropped tops, shorter sleeves, or higher hems are common
- Waistlines sit naturally or slightly higher to emphasize proportions

## Styling Tendencies

- Playful, slightly daring, sometimes subtly feminine
- Comfortable with showing skin (midriff, arms, legs)
- Effortless rather than styled-to-perfection
- Often appears slightly “underdressed” in a charming way

## Color & Material

- Warm tones, soft neutrals, or playful color accents
- Lightweight fabrics: cotton, mesh, soft knits, athletic blends
- Materials that move, stretch, or react to the body

## Presence in Scenes

- Clothes enhance motion and expression
- Fabric responds visibly to posture and movement
- Styling supports emotional readability rather than authority
