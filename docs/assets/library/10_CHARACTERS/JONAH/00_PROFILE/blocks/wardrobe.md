## Wardrobe

Jonah’s wardrobe is expressive, body-aware, and playful, with a strong emphasis on movement, comfort, and subtle provocation.

He naturally gravitates toward clothing that highlights his dancer’s physique rather than concealing it. His outfits often expose arms, midriff, or legs, but in a way that feels effortless and unselfconscious rather than performative or staged.

---

### Core principles

- clothing follows the body rather than structuring it
- silhouettes remain light, flexible, and movement-friendly
- exposure of skin is common and intentional, but casual in attitude
- outfits should feel lived-in, not styled or curated

---

### Common garments

- slightly cropped t-shirts or tanks (often sitting at or just above the waistband)
- lightweight athletic tops and soft casual basics
- short athletic shorts (mid-thigh or shorter)
- relaxed drawstring shorts or soft casual bottoms
- occasional fitted or semi-sheer tops (e.g. mesh) in more expressive looks
- rare layering; outfits are typically minimal and breathable

---

### Fit and construction

- soft, natural drape rather than rigid structure
- close-to-body but not restrictive
- waistlines often sit naturally or slightly higher, reinforcing leg length
- hems may ride up slightly with movement, especially for tops
- nothing bulky, heavy, or overly tailored

---

### Color palette

- prefers warm, lively tones over neutral greys
- common colors: soft terracotta, dusty coral, warm beige, sun-faded tones
- grounded with darker anchors: navy, charcoal, muted brown
- occasionally incorporates bold or playful colors (e.g. pinks or saturated tones) in expressive outfits
- avoids overly cold, desaturated palettes unless context requires it

---

### Style range

Jonah’s style exists on a spectrum:

- **casual / relaxed** → soft basics, loose athletic wear, understated but body-aware
- **athletic / active** → minimal, functional pieces that emphasize movement and physique
- **playful / flirtatious** → cropped silhouettes, shorter cuts, subtle skin exposure
- **bold / expressive** → occasional use of sheer fabrics, brighter colors, or more daring combinations

He moves fluidly between these modes depending on mood and context, but always maintains a sense of ease and authenticity.

---

### Styling notes

- outfits should never feel like costumes or fashion editorials
- avoid excessive layering, accessories, or complex styling
- avoid rigid, formal, or heavily structured clothing
- the overall impression should be effortless, tactile, and slightly mischievous

Jonah wears clothes the same way he moves: naturally, expressively, and without overthinking them.
