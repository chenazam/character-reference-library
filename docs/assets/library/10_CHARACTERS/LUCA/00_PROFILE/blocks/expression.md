Default expression:

- soft neutral expression
- relaxed eyes
- calm, open demeanor

Smile:

- warm, gentle smile
- subtle and natural
- appears easily without exaggeration

Emotional tone:

- open
- warm
- slightly shy or reserved

Expression behavior:

- expressions should feel natural and unforced
- avoid exaggerated emotional intensity
- maintain softness in the eyes and mouth

Avoid:

- sharp or intense expressions
- overly confident or dominant facial reads
- exaggerated smiles or stylization
