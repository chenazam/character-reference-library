Expression:

- default expression is neutral, controlled, and slightly guarded
- emotional changes are subtle and primarily visible in the eyes
- mouth movement is minimal and rarely fully expressive

Expression character:

- inward-facing rather than outwardly expressive
- observant rather than reactive
- controlled rather than warm
- carries a quiet sense of tension or thoughtfulness
- maintain subtle tension in the mouth at rest
- avoid overly relaxed or soft neutral expressions

Typical reads:

- neutral (baseline)
- slight curiosity (eyes only)
- restrained skepticism
- minimal amusement (barely lifted corner of the mouth)
- quiet focus

Expression behavior:

- expressions should feel contained, not broadcast
- emotion should appear as micro-adjustments, not full facial shifts
- intensity comes from stillness, not exaggeration

Avoid:

- wide smiles or visible teeth
- exaggerated expressions
- playful or flirty baseline reads
- overly sad or vulnerable collapse unless explicitly intended
- blank expressionlessness (he is controlled, not empty)
