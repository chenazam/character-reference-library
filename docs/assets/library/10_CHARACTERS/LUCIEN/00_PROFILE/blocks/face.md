Face:

- narrow, refined face shape with visible bone structure
- clearly defined jawline, but not heavy or rugged
- slight cheek hollowness that adds subtle angularity
- straight, clean nose
- dark brown observant eyes with a slightly heavy-lidded, focused look
- light skin with subtle freckles across the nose and cheeks

Facial character:

- calm, controlled, and guarded by default
- emotionally restrained rather than soft or open
- observant and slightly inward-facing
- carries a quiet sense of knowing rather than warmth
- jawline should be clearly defined with visible structure, not rounded or softened
- slight cheek hollowing to reduce facial softness
- chin should be clean and structured, not small or tapered-soft

Structural priorities:

- preserve a defined but elegant jawline (not soft, not square-heavy)
- maintain facial narrowness and slight angularity
- keep subtle tension in the face — avoid over-relaxation
- preserve freckles as identity detail

Avoid:

- overly soft or rounded facial structure
- “pretty boy” softness
- rugged or bulky masculinity
- exaggerated sharpness or model-like hyper-definition
- overly warm or approachable facial reads
