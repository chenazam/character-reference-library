Hair:

- dark brown naturally curly hair
- short to medium length
- soft natural volume
- irregular natural hairline
- texture should remain visible and slightly organic rather than overly groomed or artificially perfect

Hair behavior:

- curls should feel natural, lived-in, and slightly unruly in a controlled way
- shape should frame the face softly without becoming fluffy or chaotic
- maintain an understated scholarly-occult feel rather than trendy fashion-hair polish

Avoid:

- straightened or flattened hair
- buzzcuts or very short cropped styles
- overly sharp barbered styling
- generic polished “male model” hair
