Outfit (Signature — Dark Layered):

Core aesthetic:

- dark, understated, slightly poetic
- soft layered silhouette
- minimal, non-flashy styling
- focus on face and neckline

Upper layers:

- black hoodie
- soft fabric
- slightly oversized but controlled fit
- hood resting naturally behind neck
- no logos or graphics

- black leather jacket
- mid-length (hip length)
- worn matte leather, not glossy
- relaxed fit, not structured
- worn open

Inner layer:

- black crewneck shirt (optional, minimal, no print)

Accessories:

- thin chain necklace with small crescent moon pendant
- subtle secondary charm (minimal detail)
- thin leather bracelets with minimal metal accents
- a few simple rings (not chunky)

Lower body:

- black slim-fit pants
- straight/slim silhouette
- not tight, not loose
- matte fabric, no shine

Footwear:

- black leather boots
- ankle height
- slim profile
- matte, slightly worn finish
- minimal detailing, no heavy hardware

Constraints:

- avoid bulky or chunky footwear
- avoid bright colors or white soles
- footwear must preserve a light, slender silhouette

Styling constraints:

- do not add bulk to shoulders or torso
- preserve slender body silhouette
- avoid heavy layering thickness
- avoid fashion exaggeration or streetwear styling

Overall read:

- quiet, dark, introspective
- soft presence rather than dominant
- grounded, natural, not stylized
