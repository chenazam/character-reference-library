Outfit (Signature — Occult Layered):

Core aesthetic:

- dark, understated, slightly poetic
- modern occult practitioner (not punk, not streetwear)
- soft layered silhouette with vertical emphasis
- minimal, intentional styling
- focus on face, neckline, and symbolic detail

Upper layers:

- black hoodie
- soft fabric
- slightly oversized but controlled fit
- hood resting naturally behind neck
- no logos or graphics

- long dark coat
- mid-thigh to knee length (longer than a standard jacket)
- matte wool or soft structured fabric (not leather)
- clean vertical fall, not bulky
- relaxed but intentional fit
- worn open to preserve layering visibility

Inner layer:

- black crewneck or soft knit shirt
- matte fabric, no print
- optional slight texture (subtle, not decorative)

Accessories:

- thin chain necklace with small occult pendant
  (crescent moon, pentagram, or subtle sigil)
- pendant must feel personal and understated, not decorative
- optional second very fine chain (layered, minimal)

- a few simple rings (thin, not chunky)
- optional subtle bracelet (leather or metal, minimal)

Neckline layering:

- allow subtle layering variation around the neckline
- hoodie, shirt, and necklace should not align perfectly flat
- introduce slight natural offset and depth between layers
- maintain clean silhouette while avoiding perfectly stacked alignment

Lower body:

- black slim-fit pants
- straight/slim silhouette
- not tight, not loose
- matte fabric, no shine

Footwear:

- black leather boots
- ankle height
- slim profile
- matte, slightly worn finish
- minimal detailing, no heavy hardware

Constraints:

- avoid bulky or chunky footwear
- avoid bright colors or white soles
- footwear must preserve a light, slender silhouette

- avoid biker or punk-coded elements (no leather jacket as primary outer layer)
- avoid streetwear or fashion-forward exaggeration
- avoid dramatic fantasy styling (no robes, no costume elements)

Styling constraints:

- preserve slender body silhouette
- no added bulk in shoulders or torso
- layering must remain light and vertical, not heavy
- all elements should feel naturally worn, not styled for display

Material realism:

- fabrics must show subtle natural irregularities
- avoid perfectly smooth, uniform surfaces
- introduce slight softness, wear, and lived-in texture
- hoodie fabric should appear slightly worn and softened
- coat fabric should have natural variation, not perfectly flat matte

- avoid pristine, brand-new appearance
- avoid high-fashion polish or catalog-perfect finishes

Fit behavior:

- clothing fit should feel natural, not tailored to perfection
- allow slight looseness and organic drape in fabric
- avoid overly clean, tension-perfect silhouettes
- maintain slender profile while introducing minor natural variation

Imperfection constraints:

- imperfections must remain subtle and controlled
- avoid distressed, damaged, or stylized grunge elements
- avoid heavy wrinkles, tears, or exaggerated wear
- overall read must remain clean but naturally lived-in

Overall read:

- quiet, dark, introspective
- grounded and slightly mysterious
- reads as someone who practices, not performs
- subtle occult presence integrated into everyday clothing
