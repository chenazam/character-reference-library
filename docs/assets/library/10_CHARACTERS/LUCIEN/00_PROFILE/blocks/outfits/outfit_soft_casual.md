Outfit (Soft Casual — Off-Duty):

Core aesthetic:

- dark, understated, intimate
- softer and more relaxed than the signature outfit
- minimal, natural, unstyled appearance
- feels personal and lived-in rather than composed

Upper layers:

- soft dark t-shirt or long-sleeve
- washed black or charcoal tone
- matte fabric with slight natural wear
- no print, no graphics

- optional lightweight layer:
  - soft zip hoodie or cardigan
  - unstructured, slightly loose fit
  - worn open or naturally draped
  - no heavy outerwear, no coat

Accessories:

- same pendant as signature outfit (must remain consistent)
- thin chain, naturally resting
- optional minimal rings (same set as signature, no additions)

Lower body:

- dark relaxed-fit pants or soft denim
- slightly looser than signature (but not baggy)
- natural drape, no sharp structure
- matte, slightly worn fabric

Footwear:

- black leather boots OR minimal dark shoes
- same footwear language as signature
- slightly softer, less structured appearance acceptable

Constraints:

- avoid structured outerwear (no coats, no leather jackets)
- avoid sharp tailoring or rigid silhouettes
- avoid bright colors or strong contrast
- avoid sporty elements (no sneakers unless extremely minimal and dark)

Styling constraints:

- preserve slender silhouette without enforcing tight fit
- allow slightly more looseness and natural drape
- layering must feel incidental, not constructed
- clothing should appear casually worn, not styled

Material realism:

- fabrics must show subtle wear and softness
- avoid pristine or brand-new appearance
- maintain controlled imperfection (no heavy distressing)

Overall read:

- quiet, introspective, slightly withdrawn
- softer and more vulnerable than the signature outfit
- feels like a private version of the same person
- consistent identity, reduced intensity
