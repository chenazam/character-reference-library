Style:

Lucien’s aesthetic is occult, scholarly, and subtly gothic.

His clothing and styling should feel intelligent, restrained, and ritual-aware rather than flashy, decadent, or costume-like. The overall impression should be arcane and refined, with quiet intention in layering, silhouette, texture, and accessories.
His aesthetic should suggest quiet involvement with occult or ritual practices, not just interest. The styling should feel lived-in and intentional, as if each piece has meaning or history.

Preferred design language:

- dark layered garments
- soft structured silhouettes
- elegant long lines
- high collars
- antique jewelry
- rings
- talismans
- muted symbolic details
- soft textured fabrics such as wool and velvet
- antique-metal accents

Color direction:

- black
- charcoal
- deep plum
- muted dark neutrals
- silver-toned metal details

Avoid:

- generic casual streetwear
- bright modern basics that erase the occult-scholar read
- flashy or theatrical gothic costuming
- overly trendy styling
- anything that feels loud, performative, or costume-shop dramatic
