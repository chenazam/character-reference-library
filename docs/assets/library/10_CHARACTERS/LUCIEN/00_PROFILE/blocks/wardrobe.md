Wardrobe:

Lucien’s wardrobe should support an occult-scholar identity with subtle gothic influence and a wearable modern silhouette.

Core wardrobe principles:

- dark layered clothing
- refined but soft silhouettes
- understated structure
- tactile materials
- muted ritual-inspired detailing
- antique accessories used with restraint

Typical wardrobe pieces:

- dark knitwear
- soft tailored trousers
- long coats or long outer layers
- high-collared shirts
- simple dark button-ups
- fine wool layers
- textured overshirts
- slim dark trousers
- understated boots or elegant practical footwear

Accessories:

- rings
- talisman necklace
- antique jewelry
- small symbolic pieces with arcane or ritual undertones

Wardrobe tone:

- refined
- arcane
- composed
- intelligent
- slightly enigmatic

Avoid:

- generic streetwear basics as the dominant look
- loud gothic costume styling
- casual athletic wear
- flashy luxury styling
- excessive ornament that overwhelms the character
