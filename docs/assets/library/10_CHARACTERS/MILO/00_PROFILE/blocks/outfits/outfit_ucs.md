## Milo — Outfit (UCS)

Outfit intent:

This outfit is a grounded, practical interpretation of Milo’s original design, translated into a realistic, wearable form.

The goal is to reinforce Milo’s physical, substantial presence while maintaining a clean and natural aesthetic.

Clothing description:

- A fitted short-sleeve t-shirt in off-white or soft white.
- The shirt is close-fitting and follows the body’s natural contours, clearly showing chest, shoulders, and arm structure without appearing tight or compressive.
- Fabric is matte and natural, with minimal texture and no heavy stylization.

- Athletic shorts in a muted green tone.
- Shorts have a short to mid-short inseam, exposing most of the thigh while remaining realistic and wearable.
- Fit is slightly relaxed but still structured, maintaining a clean silhouette.

- Footwear (optional depending on framing): simple low-profile athletic shoes in neutral or green tones.

Design constraints:

- No large graphic prints or logos.
- No exaggerated costume elements or accessories.
- No stylized or anime-like shapes.
- Keep all design elements subtle and grounded.

Silhouette behavior:

- Clothing must preserve and clearly communicate Milo’s mass and muscular structure.
- The shirt should not obscure chest or shoulder form.
- Shorts should emphasize strong thighs and grounded stance.

Overall impression:

The outfit should read as practical, outdoorsy, and natural — something Milo would wear casually, not as a costume.

Avoid any sense of fashion stylization or deliberate visual design.
