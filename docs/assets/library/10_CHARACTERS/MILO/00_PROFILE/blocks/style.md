## Style — Milo

Style intent:

Milo’s style is minimal, practical, and grounded in functionality. His clothing should always feel natural, wearable, and appropriate for physical activity such as gardening or outdoor work.

Core characteristics:

- Clothing is simple, clean, and utilitarian.
- No element should feel decorative, flashy, or fashion-driven.
- Fit is functional and body-aware, but never styled for display.
- Materials appear durable, breathable, and natural.

Color palette:

- Dominated by earth tones and muted natural colors:
  - greens (primary identity color)
  - off-white, beige, brown
  - muted neutrals

- Avoid high saturation or strong contrast.

Silhouette behavior:

- Clothing should support and reveal Milo’s physical build without exaggeration.
- No oversized or stylized shapes.
- No sharp tailoring or fashion silhouettes.

Styling constraints:

- No bold patterns or graphic prints.
- No visible branding or logos.
- No layered fashion styling.

Overall impression:

Milo’s clothing should feel effortless, practical, and grounded — something he wears naturally without thinking about style.
