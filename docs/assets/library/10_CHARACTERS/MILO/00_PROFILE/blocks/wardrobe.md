## Wardrobe — Milo

Wardrobe intent:

Milo’s wardrobe consists of simple, practical clothing suitable for everyday outdoor activity.

Core pieces:

- Fitted or semi-fitted t-shirts (neutral or green tones)
- Athletic or work-style shorts (short to mid length)
- Simple lightweight footwear (trainers or work-appropriate shoes)

Optional pieces:

- Light jackets or overshirts (neutral, functional)
- Basic long-sleeve shirts for layering

Material behavior:

- Matte fabrics
- Natural textures (cotton, soft blends)
- No sheen or synthetic gloss

Constraints:

- Clothing must not restrict movement or appear delicate.
- Avoid anything that feels styled, curated, or fashion-forward.
- Avoid accessories unless strictly functional.

Overall impression:

A practical, minimal wardrobe built around comfort, movement, and everyday usability.
