# EXPRESSION — NOEL

Baseline expression:

- soft, neutral, and composed
- slight natural warmth

Emotional tone:

- playful and subtly teasing
- expressive without exaggeration

Facial behavior:

- small shifts in expression rather than large changes
- eyes carry most of the emotional nuance

Signature traits:

- faint, knowing smiles
- slightly amused or curious expressions
- occasional soft “bratty” undertone

Presence:

- inviting rather than intimidating
- emotionally open but controlled

Range:

- can shift between calm, playful, and quietly affectionate
- rarely intense or aggressive

Avoid:

- hard, stoic, or emotionally closed expressions
- exaggerated or cartoonish reactions
- aggressive or confrontational facial language
