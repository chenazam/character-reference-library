# FACE — NOEL

Face shape:

- oval structure with balanced proportions
- softly defined bone structure without harsh angularity

Jaw:

- defined but not sharp
- clean structure without heavy squaring

Features:

- youthful, smooth facial structure
- no ruggedness, no weathering
- overall softness must be preserved

Eyes:

- soft, lively eyes with a slightly playful, self-aware gaze
- expression should feel engaged and subtly teasing rather than distant

Brows:

- natural, moderately defined
- not overly heavy or sharp

Lips:

- naturally full to medium fullness
- relaxed, often slightly parted or hinting at expression

Skin:

- smooth, even, youthful
- no harsh texture or exaggerated detail

Expression baseline:

- soft neutral with underlying playfulness
- can shift into subtle or playful smile easily

Avoid:

- aging the face
- sharpening features into harsh angles
- rugged, hardened, or overly masculine reinterpretation
