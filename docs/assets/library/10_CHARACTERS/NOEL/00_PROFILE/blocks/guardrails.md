# IDENTITY GUARDRAILS — NOEL

## Preserve

- soft, youthful facial structure with model-like refinement
- natural wavy hair with volume and slight curl pattern
- elongated proportions and long limbs
- lightly athletic body with subtle softness (no bulk)
- playful, confident, slightly bratty emotional tone
- fashion-aware presentation with a polished, intentional look

## Avoid

- adding heavy muscle mass or thickening the frame
- shifting into rugged, mature, or hardened masculinity
- flattening hair into short, rigid, or overly clean styles
- removing softness from face or body
- neutralizing personality into cold, distant, or expressionless presence
- generic casual styling that loses the luxury/fashion-aware edge

## Drift Risks

- drifting toward generic athletic male (too muscular, too defined)
- drifting toward “boy-next-door plain” (losing fashion identity)
- drifting toward overly soft/childlike (losing confidence and presence)
- hair losing volume and becoming flat or simplified

## Reinforcement Notes

- always re-anchor proportions through long limbs and vertical elegance
- ensure expression carries subtle playfulness, not neutrality
- maintain a sense of intentional styling even in casual looks
