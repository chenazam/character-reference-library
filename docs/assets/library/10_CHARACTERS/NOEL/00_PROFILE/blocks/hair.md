# HAIR — NOEL

Length:

- short to medium length

Structure:

- naturally wavy with slight curl pattern
- soft, airy volume throughout
- not flat, not tightly controlled

Styling:

- slightly tousled, effortless appearance
- controlled enough to feel styled, but never rigid or over-structured

Silhouette:

- adds softness and movement to the head shape
- should enhance youthful and romantic qualities

Hairline:

- natural, clean, not exaggerated

Color:

- dark brown

Behavior:

- retains volume and texture across views
- does not collapse into flat or overly neat shapes

Avoid:

- overly short or tight cuts that remove volume
- overly styled, gelled, or rigid shapes
- straightening out the natural wave completely
