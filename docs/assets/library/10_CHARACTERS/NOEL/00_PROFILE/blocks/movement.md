# MOVEMENT — NOEL

General movement:

- relaxed, fluid, and unhurried
- light and effortless rather than grounded or forceful

Posture:

- upright but not rigid
- slightly open, never tense or defensive

Gestures:

- soft, controlled, and expressive
- uses subtle movement rather than large gestures

Energy:

- calm and self-assured
- not physically imposing
- presence comes from ease, not force

Interaction style:

- comfortable in close proximity
- naturally responsive rather than initiating dominance

Physical presence:

- reads light and agile
- never heavy, planted, or aggressive

Avoid:

- sharp, forceful, or abrupt movement
- rigid or militaristic posture
- exaggerated physical dominance
