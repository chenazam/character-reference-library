# STYLE — NOEL

Overall aesthetic:

- refined, modern, fashion-aware
- clean and composed with a soft, effortless finish
- reads curated rather than casual

Visual impression:

- light, elegant, and slightly delicate
- never rugged or heavy
- avoids visual density or harsh contrast

Details:

- smooth skin rendering with subtle tonal variation
- clean lines and soft edges
- no exaggerated texture or surface noise

Hair:

- styled but natural
- soft volume with controlled shape
- slightly tousled rather than rigid

Color presence:

- favors soft neutrals and muted tones
- avoids overly saturated or aggressive color palettes

Avoid:

- rugged, gritty, or overly masculine styling
- heavy contrast or harsh shadowing
- messy or unstructured presentation
