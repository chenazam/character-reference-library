# WARDROBE — NOEL

General style:

- fashion-forward and curated
- blends high-end luxury with modern casual
- clearly styled, never accidental

Silhouette:

- fitted or intentionally tailored
- emphasizes his elongated, slender frame
- avoids bulky or oversized shaping

Materials:

- soft, high-quality fabrics
- smooth textures with subtle structure
- premium finishes over utilitarian materials

Outfit tendencies:

- clean layering with intentional composition
- minimal but deliberate accessories
- pieces feel selected, not random

Influence:

- originally rooted in modern streetwear
- now elevated with luxury elements and designer influence

Impression:

- polished but effortless
- styled without appearing try-hard
- slightly indulgent, reflecting access to wealth

Avoid:

- rugged, workwear, or utilitarian styles
- heavy layering that obscures silhouette
- overly sporty or performance-driven clothing
