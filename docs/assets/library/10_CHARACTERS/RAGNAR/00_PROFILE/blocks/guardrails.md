Always preserve:

- extremely tall height and imposing scale
- massive, dense muscular build
- strongly shoulder-dominant frame
- extremely broad shoulders and thick chest mass
- square facial structure and strong jawline
- long blond shoulder-length hair
- short blond beard
- grounded, stable posture
- calm, controlled expression
- rugged utilitarian aesthetic

Avoid:

- lean, slender, or lightly athletic body types
- reducing shoulder width or upper-body mass
- shortening or simplifying the hair silhouette
- removing the beard
- softening facial structure into a narrow or youthful appearance
- ornate or decorative fantasy styling
- lightweight or fashionable modern clothing that undermines physical presence
