outfit_modern_elevated:

Intent:
Ragnar styled through Lucien’s aesthetic lens. The outfit should translate Ragnar’s imposing, physically dominant presence into a refined, modern, arcane-adjacent silhouette without diminishing his scale or strength.

Silhouette:

- strong V-shaped upper body emphasis
- fitted base layers highlighting chest, shoulders, and arms
- long vertical outer layer worn open to preserve body readability
- clean, uninterrupted leg line
- no bulky or oversized elements

Base Layer:

- fitted black or charcoal shirt
- slightly elevated neckline (structured crew or subtle mock neck)
- soft matte or fine-textured fabric

Mid Layer:

- structured overshirt or light jacket
- soft tailoring with minimal closures
- subtle seam structure referencing functional construction

Outer Layer:

- long coat (mid-thigh to knee length)
- soft structured shoulders
- clean vertical fall, worn open
- minimal lapel or high-collar hybrid
- matte wool or heavy fabric with slight weight

Pants:

- slim tailored black pants
- clean taper, minimal stacking
- structured but flexible fit

Footwear:

- black leather boots
- sleek, minimal, slightly elevated sole
- grounded, utilitarian silhouette

Accessories:

- 1–2 understated rings (silver or dark metal)
- single talisman necklace
- minimal additional elements

Color Palette:

- black, charcoal, deep neutral tones
- optional muted deep plum accents
- restrained, non-contrasting palette

Material Direction:

- wool, heavy cotton, matte structured fabrics
- subtle texture over visual complexity

Avoid:

- oversized or loose silhouettes
- generic streetwear (hoodies, athletic wear)
- flashy gothic or costume styling
- bright modern fashion colors
- excessive layering or ornamentation
