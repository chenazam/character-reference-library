outfit_modern_lucien_styled_edge:

Intent:
A more assertive Lucien-styled interpretation of Ragnar, emphasizing his physical dominance through body-forward styling, restrained harness elements, and controlled tension in silhouette. The outfit should feel intentional, slightly provocative, and structurally aware without becoming costume-like.

Silhouette:

- body-centered, minimal layering
- strong V-shaped upper body emphasis
- visible chest, shoulders, and waist taper
- clothing interacts with the body rather than covering it
- tension lines created through straps or fitted construction

Base Layer:

- ultra-fitted black or charcoal shirt or sleeveless top
- second-skin fit with subtle texture
- slightly deeper neckline, clean and intentional

Harness Layer:

- dark leather or matte strap system across torso
- minimal, structured, non-decorative
- asymmetrical or lightly symmetrical layout
- integrated into outfit rather than sitting on top

Pants:

- slim to tight tailored black pants
- strong thigh and leg definition
- subtle paneling or seam structure
- optional minimal strap or reinforcement elements

Footwear:

- black boots with slightly more aggressive structure
- clean but assertive silhouette
- subtle strap or panel detailing allowed

Accessories:

- talisman necklace
- 2–3 rings
- optional minimal wrist accessory

Color Palette:

- black, charcoal, deep muted tones
- no bright accents
- tonal variation through material rather than color

Material Direction:

- matte fabrics, leather, structured textiles
- subtle texture over visual noise

Avoid:

- long coats or silhouette-breaking layers
- generic modern menswear
- fetish or overtly stylized harness aesthetics
- fantasy armor elements
- excessive ornamentation
