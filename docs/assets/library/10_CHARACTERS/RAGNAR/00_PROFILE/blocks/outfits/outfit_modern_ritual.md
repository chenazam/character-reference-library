outfit_modern_lucien_styled_ritual:

Intent:
A ritual-influenced Lucien-styled outfit for Ragnar that moves beyond conventional fashion into structured, intentional design. The outfit should feel like Ragnar has been shaped, marked, and refined through Lucien’s arcane aesthetic, while remaining grounded in modern materials and wearable construction.

Silhouette:

- body-forward with strong upper-body emphasis
- asymmetrical structure across torso and waist
- diagonal or offset visual flow
- minimal layering with controlled visual hierarchy
- clothing interacts with the body and creates tension lines

Base Layer:

- ultra-fitted black or charcoal shirt or sleeveless top
- second-skin fit
- subtle texture (fine rib or soft patterning)

Harness System:

- asymmetrical strap structure across chest and torso
- one shoulder more visually weighted than the other
- diagonal chest strap with off-center waist anchoring
- matte leather or structured textile materials
- integrated, non-decorative, purposeful placement

Arcane Detailing:

- subtle stitched or embroidered lines forming geometric or sigil-like structures
- tonal, low-contrast execution
- visible only under close inspection or good lighting
- integrated into seams and paneling rather than applied on top

Upper Layer (optional):

- minimal cropped or asymmetrical outer element
- partial coverage only
- does not obscure torso structure

Pants:

- slim, structured black pants
- paneling that echoes torso asymmetry
- subtle diagonal seams or reinforcement elements
- strong leg definition without exaggeration

Footwear:

- black boots with slightly heavier construction
- grounded, durable appearance
- subtle ruggedness allowed

Accessories:

- talisman necklace (primary)
- 2–4 rings
- optional secondary chain or small pendant
- all elements restrained and intentional

Color Palette:

- black, charcoal, deep muted tones
- tonal variation through material and texture
- no bright or high-contrast accents

Material Direction:

- matte fabrics, leather, structured textiles
- subtle texture and layering of materials
- no glossy or decorative finishes

Avoid:

- explicit or visible magical symbols
- glowing effects or fantasy elements
- costume-like or theatrical design
- excessive ornamentation
- breaking the grounded modern realism of the outfit

Refinement:

- increase body emphasis through tighter, second-skin fabric behavior
- reduce visual thickness of the shirt, making it read closer to skin
- deepen arm openings or sleeve cut to expose more shoulder and upper chest
- slightly reduce strap thickness and align straps more closely with anatomical lines
- introduce subtle side cut-ins or contour emphasis along the torso
- maintain grounded realism and avoid any explicit or costume-like appearance
