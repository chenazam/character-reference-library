Wardrobe (UCS armor state):

Ragnar wears a grounded, battle-worn armor ensemble that reads as a warrior from a different time, clearly out of place in a modern setting.

The outfit is defined by strong, readable shapes and controlled material variation, avoiding excessive detail while preserving a heavy, imposing silhouette.

Upper body:

- fitted leather torso piece in deep desaturated red, structured and reinforced, with visible paneling but no excessive segmentation
- dark leather and metal shoulder protection with broad, simplified shapes (no intricate layering or micro-detail)
- forearm guards / gauntlets in worn dark metal or leather, solid and functional in design

Outer layer:

- long, heavy cloak or mantle in dark charcoal or black
- cloak hangs from the shoulders with clear weight and vertical flow
- edges slightly worn or distressed, but not overly tattered
- silhouette of the cloak must remain clean and readable (no excessive tearing or noise)

Lower body:

- dark, structured pants reinforced with simplified armor plating
- armor panels are broad and functional, not highly segmented or decorative
- overall leg silhouette remains heavy and stable, supporting the upper-body mass

Footwear:

- heavy, armored boots in dark leather and metal
- thick soles and reinforced structure
- designed for durability and weight, not elegance

Materials and color:

- palette: black, charcoal, deep desaturated red
- materials: worn leather, matte metal, minimal reflectivity
- surfaces show light wear and use, but avoid excessive texture noise

Accessories:

- minimal and functional only
- optional sword (sheathed), but not required for the UCS panel
- no decorative or ornamental elements that do not serve structure or function

Fit and silhouette:

- outfit must reinforce Ragnar’s extreme scale and shoulder width
- strong upper-body dominance with clear vertical weight from cloak
- clean, bold silhouette that remains readable at a distance

Style constraints:

- avoid high-fantasy ornamentation, filigree, or decorative complexity
- avoid modern clothing elements entirely
- avoid overly intricate armor segmentation or noisy detailing
- prioritize clarity, weight, and repeatability over realism detail

Goal:

- produce a reusable, identity-defining armor look that consistently reads as Ragnar’s default state
- maintain strong silhouette recognition across different scenes and generations
