Ragnar’s visual aesthetic is rugged and utilitarian with subtle military and gothic influences.

His clothing emphasizes function, durability, and physical protection rather than decoration. Materials typically include leather and aged metal, with surfaces that appear worn and practical.

The color palette is dominated by dark, muted tones such as black and dark brown, with occasional accents of deep red or steel.

Overall styling should feel grounded, practical, and battle-ready rather than ornate or ceremonial.
