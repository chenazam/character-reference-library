Ragnar wears clothing and equipment that emphasize durability, protection, and functional design.

Typical elements include layered leather garments, reinforced armor pieces, belts, and heavy outer layers such as cloaks.

Silhouettes should reinforce his broad shoulders and massive upper body, avoiding anything that reduces or obscures his physical scale.

Outfits should appear practical, worn, and suited for physical activity rather than decorative or stylized.
