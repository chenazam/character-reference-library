# Pair core

- relationship type: romantic / intimate partnership (light, natural, mutually engaged)

---

## Baseline tone

- warm, relaxed, and naturally connected
- interaction feels effortless rather than intense
- energy is shared outward and inward, not isolated

---

## Dynamic polarity

- grounded, stabilizing presence <-> light, expressive presence

---

## Emotional structure

- one partner provides stability and physical grounding (Milo)
- one partner brings lightness, expression, and visual energy (Aaron)

- dynamic is about balance rather than control

---

## Connection quality

- comfortable, familiar, and physically natural
- connection feels steady and continuous rather than consuming
- underlying affection is constant but not overwhelming

---

## Public vs private split

- public:
  - relaxed, open, and natural interaction
  - visible comfort and familiarity
  - light physical closeness without tension

- private:
  - more openly affectionate
  - increased physical closeness and softness
  - emotional warmth becomes more direct

---

## Important

- interaction must feel mutual and balanced, not dominant/submissive
- avoid intensity-driven or controlling dynamics
- connection should feel natural, grounded, and easy
