# Interaction behavior

## Baseline behavior (public)

- physically comfortable around each other
- interaction is relaxed and unforced
- attention flows naturally between each other and the environment

---

## Milo (grounded partner)

- steady, calm, and physically present
- does not seek attention
- anchors interaction through stability and consistency

---

## Aaron (expressive partner)

- more outwardly expressive and reactive
- engages more with environment initially
- naturally returns attention toward Milo

---

## Touch behavior

- more frequent than intense pairings, but casual and natural
- touch feels habitual rather than deliberate

---

## Core interactions

- light contact at arm, shoulder, or back
- standing close without tension
- casual leaning or shared space
- relaxed physical alignment rather than guided movement

---

## Escalation behavior

- escalation is soft and gradual

- Milo initiates through:
  - proximity
  - steady presence
  - subtle contact

- Aaron responds by:
  - leaning into contact
  - maintaining closeness
  - softening outward attention

---

## Private behavior

- more openly affectionate and physically close
- interaction becomes softer rather than more intense
- both partners remain balanced and engaged

---

## Avoid

- controlling or directive interaction
- high-intensity or tension-driven escalation
- emotionally distant or neutral behavior
