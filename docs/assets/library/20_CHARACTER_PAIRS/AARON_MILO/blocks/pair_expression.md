# Expression relationship

## Baseline tone (public)

- relaxed
- warm
- lightly expressive
- emotionally open without intensity

---

## Emotional pairing

- Milo:
  - calm, soft, and steady expression
  - subtle warmth rather than overt emotion
  - reads as grounded and reassuring

- Aaron:
  - more expressive and playful baseline
  - light smiles or soft, engaged expressions
  - shifts toward calm when focused on Milo

---

## Gaze behavior

- frequent but natural eye contact
- Aaron may look outward more often, but returns easily
- Milo’s gaze is steady and consistent

---

## Focused moments

- when attention aligns:
  - expressions soften rather than intensify
  - emotional tone becomes calm and connected
  - no sharp shift into intensity

---

## Private tone

- more openly affectionate expressions
- Aaron becomes softer and more focused
- Milo shows slightly more visible warmth

---

## Avoid

- overly intense or charged expressions
- dominance-driven emotional contrast
- identical emotional tone without variation
