# Pair core

- relationship type: romantic / intimate partnership (exclusive, intensity-driven)

---

## Baseline tone

- highly focused, intense, and emotionally charged
- less performative than other pairings
- energy is directed inward toward each other rather than outward

---

## Dynamic polarity

- magnetic, controlling presence <-> expressive, attention-driven presence

---

## Emotional structure

- one partner naturally draws attention (Jasper)
- one partner redirects and concentrates that attention (Alek)

- dynamic is not about sharing attention, but about narrowing it

---

## Connection quality

- strong emotional and physical pull
- relationship feels consuming rather than socially expansive
- tension and desire are constant underlying elements

---

## Public vs private split

- public:
  - more restrained than other pairings
  - visible tension beneath controlled behavior
  - subtle but unmistakable connection

- private:
  - significantly more intense and physically driven
  - stronger dominance / submission expression
  - emotional connection remains present beneath intensity

---

## Important

- dominance must read as controlled and intentional, never aggressive or unstable
- connection must feel mutually desired and reciprocated
- avoid purely social or playful interpretation — this dynamic is focused and charged
