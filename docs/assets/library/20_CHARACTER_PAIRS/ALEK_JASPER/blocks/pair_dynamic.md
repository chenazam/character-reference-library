# Interaction behavior

## Baseline behavior (public)

- physically aware of each other at all times
- interaction is less playful, more deliberate
- attention remains primarily between partners

---

## Jasper (expressive partner)

- initially engages with environment and external attention
- naturally expressive and socially reactive
- gradually shifts focus toward Alek in his presence

---

## Alek (dominant partner)

- does not compete for attention directly
- controls interaction through proximity, gaze, and timing
- redirects Jasper’s attention through subtle but decisive actions

---

## Touch behavior

- less frequent than Blake/Jasper, but more intentional
- each touch carries meaning and direction

---

## Core interactions

- guiding by waist or lower back
- subtle pulling into personal space
- positioning that reduces distance gradually rather than abruptly
- controlled physical framing (placing himself in Jasper’s space)

---

## Escalation behavior

- escalation is slower, but more intense

- Alek initiates escalation through:
  - reduced distance
  - sustained eye contact
  - deliberate physical contact

- Jasper responds by:
  - shifting focus fully onto Alek
  - reducing outward attention
  - matching intensity rather than amplifying playfulness

---

## Private behavior

- significantly more physically expressive
- Jasper becomes more focused and receptive
- Alek becomes more assertive and physically directive

---

## Avoid

- overly playful or socially performative interaction
- constant outward engagement from Jasper
- passive or neutral interaction between partners
