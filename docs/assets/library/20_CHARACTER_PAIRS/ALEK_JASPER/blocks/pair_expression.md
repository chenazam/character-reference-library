# Expression relationship

## Baseline tone (public)

- restrained
- focused
- subtly charged
- emotionally contained with visible tension

---

## Emotional pairing

- Alek:
  - controlled, intent expression
  - subtle smirk or focused gaze
  - reads as aware and deliberate

- Jasper:
  - initially expressive and open
  - gradually becomes more focused and reactive
  - expression shifts toward intensity rather than playfulness

---

## Gaze behavior

- strong eye contact when engaged
- Jasper may initially look outward, but is drawn back toward Alek
- Alek frequently watches Jasper rather than the environment

---

## Focused moments

- when attention locks:
  - expressions become more intense and less performative
  - external awareness fades
  - emotional energy becomes directed and contained

---

## Private tone

- more open and emotionally exposed expressions
- Jasper shows vulnerability and focus
- Alek shows subtle warmth beneath control

---

## Avoid

- overly playful or teasing expressions in public
- both characters expressing identical emotional tone
- emotionally flat or disconnected expressions
