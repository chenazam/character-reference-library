# Pose relationship rules

## Distance

- close proximity preferred
- distance often reduced gradually rather than immediately

---

## Orientation

- strong inward orientation
- bodies frequently angled toward each other
- Alek often occupies Jasper’s space rather than standing beside him

---

## Hierarchy

- Alek reads as controlling presence through positioning and stillness
- Jasper reads as reactive and dynamically aligned

---

## Positioning

- Alek:
  - grounded, stable, slightly dominant positioning
  - may stand slightly closer or subtly block external space

- Jasper:
  - more dynamic posture
  - may lean, turn, or shift toward Alek
  - often slightly more visually open

---

## Contact rules

- physical contact is intentional and directional
- default to a single clear point of contact

---

## Contact examples

- hand at waist or lower back
- guiding touch
- subtle pull into proximity
- controlled framing of space

---

## Framing

- avoid symmetrical or neutral spacing
- allow overlap and spatial tension
- composition should feel slightly compressed and intimate

---

## Avoid

- wide neutral spacing between characters
- mirrored or equal positioning
- overly casual or disconnected poses
