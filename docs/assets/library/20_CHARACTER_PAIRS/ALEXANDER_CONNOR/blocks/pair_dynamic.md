# Interaction behavior

## Baseline behavior (public)

- consistent awareness of each other
- interaction is calm, soft, and minimally performative
- Connor remains subtly oriented toward Alexander

---

## Connor (receptive partner)

- naturally warm, attentive, and emotionally open
- tends to orient physically and emotionally toward Alexander
- responds with quiet focus and soft engagement
- appears relaxed and at ease within close proximity

---

## Alexander (grounding partner)

- calm, composed, and observant
- interaction is shaped through presence, positioning, and timing
- does not seek attention — attention naturally settles around him
- maintains steady awareness of Connor

---

## Touch behavior

- touch is gentle, controlled, and grounding
- physical contact reinforces connection and proximity rather than intensity

---

## Core interactions

- hand at shoulder or upper back
- arm around waist
- light, steady contact maintaining closeness
- subtle gestures that keep both figures visually connected

---

## Interaction progression

- interaction becomes more focused through:
  - reduced distance
  - sustained but calm gaze
  - consistent, quiet physical contact

- Connor responds by:
  - settling further into proximity
  - maintaining relaxed attention
  - becoming more still and present

---

## Private behavior

- more inward and emotionally focused
- interaction feels contained and self-sufficient
- both characters become less outwardly expressive and more internally engaged

---

## Avoid

- chaotic or playful interaction
- equal or mirrored positioning
- visible resistance or conflict
- exaggerated intensity or dramatic escalation
