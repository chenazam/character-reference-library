# Expression relationship

## Baseline tone (public)

- soft
- calm
- subtly attentive
- emotionally warm with quiet underlying focus

---

## Emotional pairing

- Alexander:
  - controlled, observant expression
  - subtle focus or restrained warmth
  - reads as aware and steady

- Connor:
  - open and gentle expression
  - warm, relaxed, and emotionally available
  - expression softens further in Alexander’s presence

---

## Gaze behavior

- Connor frequently looks toward Alexander
- Alexander often watches Connor rather than the environment
- eye contact is steady but not confrontational

---

## Focused moments

- when attention settles:
  - Connor’s expression becomes more still and focused
  - Alexander’s gaze becomes more intent but calm
  - emotional energy becomes inward and contained

---

## Private tone

- Connor shows increased softness and openness
- Alexander shows subtle warmth beneath composure
- expressions remain intimate and focused rather than dramatic

---

## Avoid

- identical emotional tone between characters
- overly intense or aggressive expressions
- exaggerated emotional reactions
- emotionally flat or disconnected expressions
