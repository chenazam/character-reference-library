# Pose relationship rules

## Distance

- close proximity preferred
- distance remains minimal and naturally reduced

---

## Orientation

- strong inward orientation
- Connor consistently angles toward Alexander
- Alexander maintains a stable position within the shared space

---

## Hierarchy

- Alexander reads as stable and grounding
- Connor reads as softer and more adaptive in positioning

---

## Positioning

- Alexander:
  - upright, composed, and steady
  - maintains a clear, stable posture

- Connor:
  - slightly inward posture
  - may lean or orient toward Alexander
  - appears visually open and receptive

---

## Contact rules

- physical contact is soft, natural, and consistent
- contact reinforces closeness rather than emphasis

---

## Contact examples

- hand at shoulder or upper back
- arm around waist
- light resting contact
- gentle shared space framing

---

## Framing

- avoid perfectly symmetrical positioning
- allow slight overlap and closeness
- composition should feel intimate but calm

---

## Avoid

- wide spacing between characters
- mirrored or equal positioning
- aggressive or forceful physical interaction
- rigid or overly staged poses
