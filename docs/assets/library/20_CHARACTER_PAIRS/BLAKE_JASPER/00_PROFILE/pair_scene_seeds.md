# Pair Scenario Prompts — Blake & Jasper

---

## 1. Social Spotlight / Performance

- Jasper at the center of attention in a social setting, confidently engaging with others, while Blake watches nearby with a relaxed, proud smirk

- Jasper interacting playfully with others, with Blake standing close behind or beside him, subtly maintaining control through presence

- a moment where Jasper thrives in attention while Blake remains composed, clearly aware and in control of the situation

---

## 2. Teasing & Provocation

- Jasper teasing Blake directly, smiling or smirking as he pushes his buttons

- Jasper leaning in close with a playful, provocative expression while Blake responds with controlled intensity

- a moment of mutual teasing where both are clearly enjoying the tension and interaction

---

## 3. Possessive Contact

- Blake pulling Jasper closer by the waist or hips in a confident, possessive gesture

- Blake guiding Jasper’s movement through a firm but controlled touch

- Jasper leaning into Blake’s hold, fully comfortable and engaged

---

## 4. Power Couple Presence

- both standing together in a social environment, visually strong and confident, sharing attention as a pair

- Blake and Jasper positioned side by side, both commanding presence in different ways

- a moment where they appear fully synchronized in energy and confidence

---

## 5. Private Softness

- Jasper sitting in Blake’s lap, relaxed and quieter, taking a break from social energy

- a softer moment where Blake’s posture remains dominant but his expression becomes more caring

- Jasper leaning into Blake with a more vulnerable or relaxed expression

---

## 6. Controlled Escalation

- playful interaction shifting into a more focused, charged moment through eye contact and reduced distance

- Blake closing the space between them, shifting tone from teasing to intensity

- Jasper responding immediately to that shift, becoming more attentive and aligned

---

## Usage Notes

- emphasize energy and confidence over restraint
- maintain strong chemistry and interaction at all times
- avoid neutral or low-energy compositions
