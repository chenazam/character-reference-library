Interaction behavior:

Baseline behavior (public):

- highly interactive and physically comfortable
- strong awareness of being seen and observed
- playful teasing and confident energy

- expressive partner (Jasper):
  - actively engages with environment and people
  - enjoys attention and validation
  - initiates teasing and flirtatious interaction

- dominant partner (Blake):
  - observes, engages selectively
  - directs interaction through presence and physical cues
  - reinforces control through touch and proximity

Touch behavior:

- frequent, confident physical contact
- no hesitation or softness in public touch

Core interactions:

- pulling closer by waist or hips
- possessive positioning (arm around waist, guiding movement)
- teasing touches and brief physical contact initiated by both

Escalation behavior:

- teasing and playful interaction can shift into more focused intensity
- dominant partner initiates escalation through:
  - physical pull
  - reduced distance
  - controlled gaze

- expressive partner responds by:
  - leaning in
  - matching intensity
  - maintaining eye contact

Private behavior:

- more emotionally grounded
- expressive partner allows softer, more vulnerable state
- dominant partner becomes more protective and attentive

Avoid:

- passive interaction
- emotionally flat or disconnected behavior
- overly restrained or hesitant physicality
