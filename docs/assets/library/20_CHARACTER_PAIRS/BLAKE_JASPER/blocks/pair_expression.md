Expression relationship:

Baseline tone (public):

- teasing
- confident
- slightly provocative
- high emotional energy

Emotional pairing:

- dominant partner:
  - confident, controlled expression
  - smirk, amusement, or focused intensity
  - visible pride or satisfaction

- expressive partner:
  - highly expressive and reactive
  - playful, teasing, or flirtatious
  - confident in being seen and desired

Gaze behavior:

- strong eye contact between partners
- expressive partner may look outward (toward others) frequently
- dominant partner often looks at expressive partner with controlled focus

Focused moments:

- when attention locks:
  - expressions become more intense and directed
  - teasing shifts toward sensual tension
  - external awareness reduces

Private tone:

- softer, more affectionate expressions
- expressive partner becomes more emotionally open
- dominant partner shows more warmth and care

Avoid:

- overly wholesome or soft expressions in public
- emotionally neutral or disconnected faces
- both characters expressing identical emotional tone
