Expression relationship:

Baseline tone:

- warm, affectionate, and connected
- emotional openness from Luca, controlled warmth from Danny

Emotional pairing:

- Danny:
  - restrained expression
  - subtle warmth
  - controlled gaze
  - occasional visible pride or satisfaction

- Luca:
  - open and emotionally readable
  - visible affection, curiosity, or amusement
  - faster and more varied expression changes

Gaze behavior:

- frequent eye contact
- Luca often looks first or more frequently
- Danny’s gaze carries more intensity when directed

Focused moments:

- when attention locks:
  - expressions become more intense but not exaggerated
  - Luca’s expression softens and focuses
  - Danny’s expression becomes more intent and controlled

Typical shared emotional tones:

- affection
- fondness
- amusement
- anticipation

Avoid:

- both characters emotionally flat
- both characters equally expressive at the same time
- mismatched emotional tone
