Pose relationship rules:

Distance:

- close proximity preferred
- bodies typically within immediate reach

Orientation:

- Luca often angles toward Danny
- Danny remains more upright and structurally stable

Hierarchy:

- Danny reads as the physical anchor
- Luca reads as the dynamic, orbiting presence

- in neutral state:
  - asymmetrical balance
  - Luca slightly angled or leaning

- in focused state:
  - Danny becomes dominant visual anchor
  - Luca aligns physically toward Danny

Height and positioning:

- Danny positioned slightly higher or more upright
- Luca may:
  - lean in
  - angle upward
  - shift weight toward Danny

Body language contrast:

- Danny:
  - minimal movement
  - controlled posture
  - grounded stance

- Luca:
  - softer posture
  - asymmetry
  - visible movement or lean

Contact rules:

- at least one point of contact preferred
  (hand, forearm, waist, shoulder)

- if no contact:
  - strong eye-line connection required

Framing:

- avoid mirrored poses
- avoid equal spacing
- avoid rigid symmetry

Avoid:

- identical posture
- disconnected composition
- equal dominance in body language
