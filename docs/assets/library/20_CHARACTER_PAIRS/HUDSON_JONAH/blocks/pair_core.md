Pair core:

- relationship type: romantic / intimate partnership

- baseline tone:
  - playful, affectionate, and socially relaxed
  - underlying constant sensual tension
  - chemistry becomes immediately apparent when attention locks between them

- dynamic polarity:
  - grounded, controlled presence <-> expressive, energetic presence

- emotional structure:
  - one stabilizes and contains
  - one expresses and amplifies

- connection quality:
  - deeply bonded and intuitive
  - strong emotional trust
  - constant awareness of each other even in social environments

- escalation pattern:
  - dynamic can shift rapidly from playful and social -> focused, intimate, and intense
  - transition is triggered through attention (eye contact, proximity, subtle cues)

Important:

- relationship must feel emotionally genuine and mutually desired
- dominance is consensual, affectionate, and grounded in trust
- avoid aggression, coercion, or emotional imbalance
