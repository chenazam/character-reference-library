Interaction behavior:

Baseline behavior:

- relaxed and socially integrated
- one partner more outwardly expressive and interactive
- the other more observant and grounded

- frequent casual proximity
- light physical contact is natural and recurring

Attention flow:

- expressive partner frequently initiates interaction
- grounded partner responds with subtle control and direction

- attention can shift instantly into full focus between them
- when focus locks, external environment becomes secondary

Initiation pattern:

- playful contact (leaning, light touch, brief affection) often initiated by expressive partner
- more grounded, deliberate physical guidance initiated by grounded partner

Escalation behavior:

- transitions from playful → focused are fast and clear
- grounded partner leads escalation through:
  - eye contact
  - subtle physical pull or repositioning
  - reduction of movement and increased stillness

- expressive partner responds immediately:
  - reduced external attention
  - increased focus and emotional intensity
  - visible shift from playful to attentive

Avoid:

- passive or emotionally neutral interaction
- disconnected or distant positioning
- equal-energy mirroring with no contrast
