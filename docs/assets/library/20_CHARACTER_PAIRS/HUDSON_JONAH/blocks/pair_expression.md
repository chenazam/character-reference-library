Expression relationship:

Baseline tone:

- warm, playful, and connected
- emotional openness from one side, controlled warmth from the other

Emotional pairing:

- grounded partner:
  - restrained expression
  - subtle warmth
  - controlled gaze
  - occasional visible pride or satisfaction

- expressive partner:
  - open and emotionally readable
  - visible affection, excitement, or curiosity
  - faster and more varied expression changes

Gaze behavior:

- frequent eye contact
- expressive partner often looks first or more frequently
- grounded partner’s gaze carries more weight when directed

Focused moments:

- when attention locks:
  - expressions become more intense but not exaggerated
  - external awareness drops
  - emotional tone shifts toward intimacy and focus

Typical shared emotional tones:

- affection
- amusement
- fondness
- anticipation

Avoid:

- both characters emotionally flat
- both characters overly expressive at the same time
- mismatched emotional tone
