Pose relationship rules:

Distance:

- close or medium proximity preferred
- bodies often within immediate reach

Orientation:

- slight inward orientation toward each other
- expressive partner often angled toward grounded partner

Hierarchy:

- grounded partner reads as stable anchor
- expressive partner reads as dynamic, orbiting presence

- in neutral state:
  - visual balance feels equal but asymmetrical

- in focused/intimate state:
  - grounded partner becomes dominant visual anchor
  - expressive partner aligns toward them physically and spatially

Height and positioning:

- grounded partner positioned slightly higher or more upright
- expressive partner may:
  - lean in
  - angle upward
  - sit or lower themselves relative to partner

Body language contrast:

- grounded partner:
  - minimal movement
  - stable posture
  - controlled positioning

- expressive partner:
  - asymmetry
  - motion or softness in posture
  - more visible gesture

Contact rules:

- at least one point of connection preferred
  (hand, arm, waist, shoulder, etc.)

- if no contact:
  - strong visual link (eye-line, orientation)

Framing:

- avoid equal spacing or mirrored poses
- avoid both characters standing rigidly or symmetrically

Avoid:

- identical posture or mirrored stance
- static, disconnected composition
