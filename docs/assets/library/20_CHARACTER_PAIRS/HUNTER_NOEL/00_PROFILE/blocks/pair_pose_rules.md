Pose and staging rules:

- Hunter maintains a stable, grounded posture:
  - upright stance
  - minimal movement
  - controlled limb positioning

- Noel’s posture is more relaxed:
  - subtle asymmetry
  - natural weight shifts
  - softer limb positioning

- physical spacing should feel intentional but natural
- neither character should block or obscure the other

- Hunter should not lean or collapse posture
- Noel should not mirror Hunter’s rigidity

- avoid mirrored posing or identical stance

- visual composition should emphasize contrast:
  - structure vs softness
  - stillness vs motion
  - symmetry vs asymmetry

- both characters must remain fully readable and distinct
