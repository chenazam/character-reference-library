# Interaction behavior

## Baseline behavior (public)

- constant awareness of each other
- interaction feels grounded and focused rather than performative

---

## Ragnar (dominant physical presence)

- presence is calm, heavy, and physically imposing
- does not need to assert dominance actively — it is inherent
- movement is minimal and controlled

---

## Lucien (lighter presence)

- more physically reactive
- adjusts position relative to Ragnar’s size and presence
- often moves within Ragnar’s space rather than beside him

---

## Physical dynamic

- interaction emphasizes contrast in size and physical presence
- Lucien’s movements feel lighter and more flexible
- Ragnar’s presence feels steady and unyielding

---

## Touch behavior

- touch reinforces scale difference

- Ragnar:
  - broad, grounding contact
  - hand placement covers more surface area

- Lucien:
  - more precise, smaller contact points
  - often reaches upward or inward

---

## Core interactions

- Lucien stepping closer into Ragnar’s space
- Ragnar enclosing or anchoring space around Lucien
- subtle guiding or stabilizing contact

---

## Escalation behavior

- escalation is quiet but intense

- Ragnar:
  - reduces space through presence rather than movement

- Lucien:
  - responds by closing distance and focusing attention upward

---

## Avoid

- equal physical presence
- interaction that ignores size difference
- both characters moving or behaving with the same physical weight
