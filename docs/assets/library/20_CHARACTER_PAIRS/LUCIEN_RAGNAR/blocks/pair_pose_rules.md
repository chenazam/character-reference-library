# Pose relationship rules

## Distance

- close proximity preferred
- distance often reduced gradually rather than immediately

---

## Orientation

- strong inward orientation
- bodies angled toward each other
- Lucien frequently turns upward toward Ragnar due to height difference

---

## Hierarchy (physical)

- Ragnar is significantly taller and larger in overall mass
- the height difference must remain clearly visible in all compositions
- Ragnar’s shoulder line sits far above Lucien’s

---

## Positioning

- Ragnar:
  - grounded, dominant vertical presence
  - occupies more visual space
  - posture reads stable and immovable

- Lucien:
  - smaller, lighter physical presence
  - often positioned slightly closer to Ragnar’s torso rather than shoulder level
  - head height typically aligns around Ragnar’s upper chest or below

---

## Contact rules

- physical contact must reinforce scale difference

---

## Contact examples

- Lucien’s hand placed on Ragnar’s chest or lower torso
- Ragnar’s arm resting across Lucien’s upper back or shoulders
- contact points should emphasize difference in reach and hand size

---

## Framing

- avoid compositions that visually equalize height
- do not align heads at the same vertical level unless explicitly required
- allow overlap where Ragnar visually dominates frame space

---

## Critical scale rules

- Lucien must not appear near Ragnar’s eye level
- Lucien should read clearly smaller in both height and width
- Ragnar’s limbs (arms, hands) should appear noticeably larger in proportion

---

## Avoid

- equal head height
- symmetrical positioning
- compositions that minimize size contrast
- perspective tricks that flatten the height difference
