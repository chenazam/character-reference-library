## Pose (FRONT — NEUTRAL)

- neutral anatomical standing pose

- arms hanging straight down with elbows fully extended
- hands slightly separated from the thighs with a small visible gap

- palms facing inward toward the body
- fingers naturally extended and relaxed (not clenched, not spread)

- legs straight
- feet shoulder-width apart

- posture upright and stable
- no contrapposto, no weight shift, no tilt
