Silhouette constraints:

- render the character as a clean, standardized technical silhouette
- prioritize silhouette clarity and cross-view consistency over pose fidelity to the reference image
- use the reference only to preserve anatomy, proportions, and body shape
- do not copy incidental pose asymmetry, weight shift, or limb placement from the reference

Pose standardization:

- reconstruct the body into a neutral, controlled standing stance appropriate for the specified view
- avoid contrapposto, hip shift, lean, twist, or relaxed asymmetry unless the recipe explicitly requests it
- maintain level shoulders, stable hips, and balanced posture
- ensure the stance reads as a reusable reference asset rather than a naturalistic moment

Readability requirements:

- arms must remain clearly separated from the torso silhouette
- legs must remain clearly readable and not merge into a single ambiguous mass
- the outer body contour must be unambiguous from head to feet
- silhouette clarity takes priority over strict faithfulness to the original pose

Proportion preservation:

- preserve the character’s actual body proportions and silhouette identity
- do not idealize, streamline, or genericize the physique
- maintain consistent shoulder width, torso taper, hip width, glute projection, thigh mass, and leg proportions across all views
