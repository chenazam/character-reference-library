Styling override:

- outfit styling takes priority over neutrality or safety

- the character must appear intentionally styled, not generically dressed

- at least one of the following must be clearly present:
  - strong color contrast
  - saturated or visually dominant garment
  - visible skin framing (open shirt, neckline)
  - noticeable styling choice

- if no clear focal point exists, the outfit is considered incorrect

- avoid resolving ambiguity with neutral or safe choices
