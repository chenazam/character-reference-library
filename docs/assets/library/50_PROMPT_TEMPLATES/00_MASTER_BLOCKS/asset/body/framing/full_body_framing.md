Full-body framing:

- the character must be fully visible from head to feet
- feet must remain fully within the frame
- no cropping of limbs or extremities is allowed

Composition:

- the full figure must be comfortably contained within the image
- maintain only slight breathing room around the subject
- do not position the character so close to the frame edges that parts risk being cut off
- the character should occupy a substantial portion of the frame while remaining fully visible

Vertical framing control:

- minimize excess empty space above the head and below the feet
- keep top and bottom margins tight but safe
- the figure should occupy most of the vertical space in the image
- avoid large unused background areas, especially below the feet
- the subject must not appear to float within excessive surrounding space

Priority:

- tight, efficient framing is preferred over overly cautious empty margins
- if the full body is visible but surrounded by excessive blank space, the result is incorrect
