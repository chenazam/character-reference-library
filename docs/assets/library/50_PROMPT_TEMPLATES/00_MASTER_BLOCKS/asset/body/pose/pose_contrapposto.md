Pose: Contrapposto weight shift

Core behavior:

- weight clearly supported by one leg
- opposite hip lowered or raised naturally to create asymmetry
- stance should feel relaxed and natural rather than posed

Alignment:

- supporting leg straight and stable
- free leg relaxed with reduced load
- hips visibly shifted relative to the shoulders
- spine forms a subtle natural curve in response to the weight shift
- shoulders remain relaxed and not overly tilted
- one arm relaxed with a casual bend, hand resting near the hip or upper thigh

Constraints:

- asymmetry must remain controlled and anatomically believable
- no exaggerated hip jut
- no extreme torso lean
- no fashion-shoot stylization
