Pose: Contrapposto S-curve stance

Core behavior:

- deliberate, aesthetically balanced asymmetrical pose
- body forms a clear S-curve through hips, spine, and shoulders
- posture reads as composed and visually structured

Alignment:

- full weight supported by one leg (clearly defined)
- supporting leg straight and stable

- opposite leg relaxed and slightly forward or outward
- hips shift clearly toward the supporting leg

- shoulders tilt in the opposite direction of the hips
- spine forms a visible, smooth S-curve

- torso upright and composed, not slouched or collapsed
- posture feels intentional rather than casual

- one arm may rest near the hip or upper thigh
- the other arm hangs naturally but with controlled placement

Silhouette goals:

- clear opposing angles between hips and shoulders
- visible S-curve through the body
- elegant, balanced asymmetry

Constraints:

- no slouching or looseness
- no symmetrical stance
- no heavy or grounded weight compression
- no casual or unstructured posture
