Pose: Grounded power stance

Core behavior:

- strong, weight-bearing stance with clear asymmetry
- body reads as heavy, planted, and physically anchored

Alignment:

- feet placed noticeably wider than shoulder width
- one leg is the primary weight-bearing leg (clearly visible)
- weight shifts strongly into that leg

- opposite leg relaxed with a slight bend
- hips shift visibly toward the weight-bearing side

- torso upright with a slight lean into the weight-bearing leg
- shoulders counterbalance subtly

- BOTH hands placed on hips
- elbows angled outward to create a strong silhouette

Silhouette goals:

- clear asymmetry between left and right sides
- visible hip shift and compression
- wide, stable base
- strong outward arm shape

Constraints:

- no symmetry or evenly distributed weight
- no contrapposto-style elegance
- no relaxed or soft posture
- no arms hanging close to torso
