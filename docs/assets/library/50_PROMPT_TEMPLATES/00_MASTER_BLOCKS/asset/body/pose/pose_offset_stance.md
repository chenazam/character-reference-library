Pose: Offset stance

Core behavior:

- feet positioned at slightly different distances without overlapping
- stance remains fully static and balanced
- body reads as relaxed and stable

Alignment:

- one foot positioned slightly ahead or to the side of the other
- legs remain close and controlled in spacing
- weight distributed naturally across both legs
- torso upright and centered
- shoulders level and relaxed
- arms relaxed naturally at the sides

Constraints:

- no motion or stepping implied
- no large separation between the legs
- no forward momentum
- no exaggerated lower-body emphasis
- no dynamic or action-oriented posture
