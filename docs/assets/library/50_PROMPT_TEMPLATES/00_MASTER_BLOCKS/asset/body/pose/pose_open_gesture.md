Pose: Open gesture stance

Core behavior:

- posture reads as open and slightly expressive
- body language extends outward from the torso

Alignment:

- stance neutral or slightly offset
- weight balanced or subtly shifted

- torso upright and open

- ONE arm extended slightly away from the body
- hand relaxed, palm neutral or slightly open

- OTHER arm relaxed near the body

Constraints:

- no dramatic or theatrical gestures
- no both arms extended
- no closed or inward posture
- extension must be clearly visible but controlled
