Pose: Relaxed casual stance

Core behavior:

- easy, natural standing posture with visible looseness
- body should read as comfortable and unforced
- stance should feel effortless rather than formal

Alignment:

- one leg may be slightly bent or offset
- hips and shoulders may show mild natural imbalance
- arms relaxed with optional light interaction such as one hand resting or lightly engaged
- torso remains upright but not rigid

Constraints:

- no strong contrapposto
- no dramatic lean
- no exaggerated slouching
- no dynamic motion or action energy
