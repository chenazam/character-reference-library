Pose: Relaxed neutral stance

Core behavior:

- casual, low-energy standing posture
- body reads as at rest, not posed
- posture feels loose but not asymmetrical

Alignment:

- weight distributed evenly across both legs
- feet placed naturally, around shoulder width

- hips remain level and centered
- no visible shift to either side

- shoulders relaxed and slightly softened, but level
- torso upright with slight natural softness (not rigid)

- arms hang loosely with minimal tension
- hands relaxed with natural finger position

Silhouette goals:

- symmetrical base with softened posture
- no strong angles or directional weight
- overall shape reads as neutral and at rest

Constraints:

- no weight shift onto one leg
- no hip displacement
- no S-curve or contrapposto structure
- no dynamic movement
- no stiffness or rigid posing
