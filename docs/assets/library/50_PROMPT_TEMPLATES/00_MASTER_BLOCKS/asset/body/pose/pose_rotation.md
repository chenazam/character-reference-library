Pose: Torso rotation

Core behavior:

- hips remain mostly forward while the torso rotates slightly
- pose should create directional engagement without becoming a full turn
- body language should feel intentional and composed

Alignment:

- torso rotated approximately 15–30 degrees relative to the hips
- head follows the torso naturally
- shoulders align with the torso rotation
- lower body remains stable and readable from the base stance

Constraints:

- no extreme twist
- no mismatch between torso and head direction
- no full profile or back-facing turn
- no dramatic fashion/editorial posing
