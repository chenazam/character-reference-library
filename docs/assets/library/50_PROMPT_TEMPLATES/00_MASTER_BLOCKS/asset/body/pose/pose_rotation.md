Pose: Torso rotation

Core behavior:

- torso rotates while the lower body remains stable
- pose creates directional engagement without becoming a full turn

Alignment:

- torso rotated approximately 30–45 degrees
- hips follow slightly but remain partially forward
- shoulders align with torso rotation

- head turned back toward the camera

- arms asymmetrical:
  - one arm relaxed at the side
  - the other arm lightly engaged (resting near waist, wrist, or opposite arm)

Constraints:

- no full profile or back-facing turn
- no symmetrical arm positions
- no hands in pockets
- no dramatic or editorial posing
- rotation must be clearly visible in torso and shoulders
