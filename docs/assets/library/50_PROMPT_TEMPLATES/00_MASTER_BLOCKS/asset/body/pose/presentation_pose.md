Presentation pose constraint:

This asset uses a natural, relaxed full-body stance instead of a strict reference pose.

Pose behavior:

- allow natural weight distribution
- subtle asymmetry is permitted
- stance should feel relaxed and grounded

Allowed:

- slight hip shift
- relaxed leg positioning
- natural arm placement (including one arm engaged or resting)
- minor shoulder angle variation

Avoid:

- perfectly symmetrical neutral reference pose
- stiff or mannequin-like posture

Restrictions:

- no exaggerated contrapposto
- no dynamic motion or action poses
- no fashion-shoot exaggeration
- no twisting that breaks anatomical clarity

Silhouette:

- body proportions and mass distribution must remain accurate
- lower-body dominance must remain clearly visible

Priority:

- natural presence
- controlled realism
- readability of the character’s physique
