Render the character as a clean, solid black silhouette.

The silhouette must be a single unified shape with no internal detail.

Do not show:

- anatomy lines
- muscle definition
- facial features
- clothing folds
- shading or gradients
- lighting or highlights

The entire figure should be filled with a uniform black tone.

Edges must be crisp, clean, and clearly readable.

The silhouette should accurately reflect the character’s true body proportions and shape, including:

- shoulder width
- torso taper
- waist shape
- hip width
- leg volume and length
- head shape and hair silhouette

Hair must be included only as part of the outer contour.

No background elements. Plain neutral background.
