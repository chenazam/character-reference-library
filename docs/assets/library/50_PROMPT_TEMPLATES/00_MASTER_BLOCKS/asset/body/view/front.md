View:
controlled front-facing full-body view

Orientation:

- body oriented directly toward the viewer
- torso, hips, and shoulders fully aligned to the front
- no rotation toward or away from the viewer
- body planes must remain frontal and symmetrical

Head alignment:

- head facing directly forward
- no independent rotation relative to the torso
- both eyes fully visible and aligned
- facial features symmetrical and centered

Pose:

- neutral standing pose
- weight evenly distributed across both legs
- no visible hip shift or contrapposto
- shoulders level and horizontally aligned
- hips level and horizontally aligned
- spine vertically aligned
- posture upright but relaxed, not rigid

Silhouette requirements:

- clean and symmetrical front silhouette
- clear separation between arms and torso
- legs clearly separated and readable
- no overlap that obscures body structure
- overall shape must be easily readable at a glance

Anatomical clarity:

- full body proportions clearly visible from head to feet
- major anatomical landmarks must be readable
- no occlusion of key structural features
- maintain consistent proportion relationships

Symmetry:

- overall symmetry must be stable and controlled
- minor natural asymmetry is allowed
- avoid perfect mirrored stiffness
- avoid visible tilt or imbalance

Constraints:

- no rotation or twist in torso or hips
- no dynamic pose or movement
- no contrapposto or weight shift
- no exaggerated posture
- no asymmetrical stance that breaks frontal alignment

Framing:

- full body clearly visible from head to feet
- centered composition
- consistent scale with other anatomy views
- no cropping of extremities
