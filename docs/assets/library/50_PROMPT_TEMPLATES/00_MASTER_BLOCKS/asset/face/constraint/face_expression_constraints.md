Expression identity constraints:

- facial expressions must not alter core facial structure
- preserve eye shape, eye spacing, and eyelid structure during expression
- preserve jawline definition and face proportions
- preserve cheekbone structure; avoid adding or removing volume
- maintain consistent nose shape and width
- maintain consistent mouth width and resting proportions

Deformation rules:

- expressions must be applied as surface-level muscle movement only
- avoid structural reinterpretation of the face
- avoid softening or rounding the face during smiling
- avoid generic “model-like” facial reshaping

Identity priority:

- maintaining recognizable facial identity is more important than expression clarity
- the same person must remain clearly identifiable across all expressions
