Expression: CHARMING / INVITING

- direct but relaxed eye contact with the viewer
- eyes slightly softened, with a hint of attentiveness and interest
- very subtle asymmetry in the mouth (faint smile or softened smirk)
- lips gently parted or lightly curved, never tense

- expression reads as socially open, warm, and slightly playful
- conveys a sense of awareness of the viewer (engaged, not neutral)
- should feel natural and effortless, not exaggerated or theatrical

Avoid:

- exaggerated smirk or cockiness (covered by SMIRK)
- overly cheerful or broad smile (covered by SMILE)
- sleepy or half-lidded disengagement
- heavy flirtation or sexualization

Goal:

- capture approachable charisma and quiet confidence
- this is the “he knows exactly what he’s doing, but keeps it subtle” expression
