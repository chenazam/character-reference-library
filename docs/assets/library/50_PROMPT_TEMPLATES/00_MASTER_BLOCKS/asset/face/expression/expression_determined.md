Expression: determined, focused intent

Core behavior:

- forward-driven, goal-oriented expression
- attention locked onto a target or objective
- emotionally controlled, but with clear internal drive
- should feel active and intentional, not passive or observational

Eyes:

- slightly narrowed with strong directional focus
- gaze locked and unwavering
- must feel engaged and purposeful, not relaxed or distant
- avoid softness or social warmth

Eyebrows:

- subtly drawn downward and slightly inward
- symmetrical engagement (not one-sided)
- must not read as skeptical, confused, or angry
- tension should be controlled and intentional, not emotional

Mouth:

- closed with light, natural pressure
- lips gently pressed together (not tight or clenched)
- no upward pull (smile) and no downward pull (sadness)
- avoid relaxed neutrality — there must be subtle engagement

Jaw:

- lightly engaged with visible structure
- no aggressive clenching, but not relaxed
- should add definition to the lower face

Head / posture (face framing only):

- very slight forward tilt of the head
- subtle sense of leaning into focus
- must remain within front-facing constraints (no angle drift)

Overall expression:

- must read as intent, drive, and internal commitment
- should feel like a moment before action or decision
- distinct from neutral through visible tension and direction
- distinct from skeptical through symmetry and lack of attitude
- distinct from anger through controlled, non-emotional tension

Constraints:

- no asymmetry in brows (avoid skeptical overlap)
- no emotional negativity (not angry, not annoyed)
- no softness or passivity (avoid vulnerable overlap)
- no relaxed neutrality (must not collapse into neutral)
- no exaggerated intensity or aggression

Identity preservation:

- maintain exact facial structure
- avoid hardening or reshaping the face
- expression must remain a surface-level muscular adjustment
