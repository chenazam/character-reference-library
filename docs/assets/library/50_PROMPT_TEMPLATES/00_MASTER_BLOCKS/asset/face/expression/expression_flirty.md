Expression: flirty, playful, inviting

- expression should feel socially directed and engaging
- conveys warmth, confidence, and subtle playful intent
- not arrogant, not smug, not skeptical

Eyes:

- slightly narrowed or relaxed, but not tense
- eyelids slightly lowered in a soft, controlled way
- gaze should feel intentional and directed, not distant or neutral
- avoid sharp or suspicious narrowing

Eyebrows:

- subtly raised or relaxed
- no strong asymmetry
- avoid furrow or skeptical tension

Mouth:

- slight, natural smirk or half-smile
- corners of the mouth gently lifted, not tight or held
- expression should feel fluid and effortless, not posed

Head / posture:

- allow a very slight head tilt or micro-angle toward the viewer
- subtle forward engagement is allowed
- avoid rigid, perfectly symmetrical alignment

Overall expression:

- must feel open, playful, and inviting
- should read as emotionally directed toward the viewer
- avoid smugness, arrogance, or detached confidence

Constraints:

- preserve facial structure and proportions
- do not soften or reshape the face
- avoid exaggerated seduction or caricature
