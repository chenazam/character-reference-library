Expression: PLAYFUL / TEASING

Core behavior:

- socially directed, teasing, lightly provocative energy
- expression feels like he is interacting with the viewer, not just reacting internally

Facial features:

- clear asymmetry in the mouth (one corner slightly raised, not a full smirk)
- eyes engaged and lively, with slight narrowing or sparkle
- brows relaxed with subtle asymmetry (not neutral, not tense)
- very slight head tilt or micro-shift to avoid stiffness

Key distinction:

- must feel outward-facing and engaging
- should suggest teasing or light challenge, not internal amusement

Avoid:

- symmetrical smile (becomes SMILE)
- controlled, self-satisfied expression (becomes SMIRK)
- neutral softness (becomes SOFT)
- exaggerated grin or cartoonish playfulness

Goal:

- capture playful social energy with a hint of teasing intent
- this is the most expressive and personality-driven slot in the sheet
