Expression: Smile

- natural, genuine smile
- expression reads as relaxed happiness

Mouth:

- mouth corners lifted naturally
- smile may show teeth depending on character
- no exaggerated widening or forced grin
- lips remain natural in shape

Eyes:

- eyes subtly engaged with the smile
- slight softening around the eyes
- no squinting or exaggerated compression

Facial tone:

- expression feels effortless and unforced
- avoid cartoonish or exaggerated smiling
- avoid artificial “perfect grin” appearance

Constraints:

- do not exaggerate the expression
- do not stylize or overemphasize emotion
- maintain anatomical realism and identity consistency
