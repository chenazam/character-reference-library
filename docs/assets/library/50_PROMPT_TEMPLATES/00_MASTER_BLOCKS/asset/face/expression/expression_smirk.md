Smirk intent:

- expression should feel self-contained, confident, and slightly inward-facing
- not socially warm, inviting, or openly engaging
- the read should be cocky, knowing, and deliberate rather than playful or friendly

Mouth:

- the smirk must be clearly asymmetrical
- one side of the mouth must be visibly raised higher than the other
- the mouth should feel slightly tight and controlled, not soft or cheerful
- avoid symmetrical smiles or expressions that read as merely pleasant

Eyes:

- maintain a neutral to slightly focused gaze
- preserve a subtle sense of awareness or appraisal
- avoid soft, inviting, teasing, or bedroom-eye expression

Head:

- keep head upright and aligned
- avoid tilt, pose-driven charm, or flirtatious posture

Constraints:

- do not neutralize or minimize the expression
- do not let the expression collapse into a nearly neutral face
- do not let the smirk read as a friendly smile or polite amusement

Priority:

- readable one-sided mouth asymmetry is mandatory
- attitude must come from asymmetry and expression, not from head pose
