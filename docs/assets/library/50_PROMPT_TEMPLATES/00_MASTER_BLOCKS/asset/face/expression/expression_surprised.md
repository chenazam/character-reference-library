Expression: surprised, alert

Core behavior:

- sudden awareness or unexpected realization
- reaction is immediate but not exaggerated
- expression should feel natural and grounded, not theatrical

Eyes:

- eyes widened noticeably
- upper eyelids raised clearly
- gaze direct and alert

Eyebrows:

- raised upward in a smooth, natural arc
- no sharp angles or tension
- avoid exaggerated lifting

Mouth:

- slightly open or parted
- lips relaxed, not stretched
- opening should be subtle and natural, not dramatic

Overall expression:

- must read as clear surprise or sudden attention
- expression should feel genuine and momentary
- avoid shock, fear, or exaggerated reaction

Constraints:

- no distortion of facial structure
- avoid cartoon-like exaggeration
- maintain anatomical realism and identity
