Neutral expression baseline:

- face in a true neutral resting state
- eyes naturally open and relaxed
- no narrowed eyes, no intense gaze, no attentiveness
- no sleepy or heavy eyelids
- eyebrows fully relaxed, with no inward angle, furrow, or lift
- mouth closed and neutral, no smile, no downturn, no lip compression
- jaw relaxed
- expression must feel passive and emotionally uncommitted

Important:

- do not inherit expression or emotional tone from reference images
- reset facial expression to a true neutral baseline
- the face should feel calm and alive, but not expressive
