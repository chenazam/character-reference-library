Neutral expression baseline:

- face in a true neutral resting state
- no active expression (no smile, no frown, no emotional bias)

- preserve the character’s natural resting facial structure
- do not artificially relax, soften, or neutralize inherent facial tension

Eyes:

- maintain natural eye shape, including slight narrowing if present
- avoid overly wide, rounded, or softened eye shapes
- preserve upper eyelid weight and structure

Eyebrows:

- maintain natural resting position
- do not flatten or neutralize brow structure

Mouth:

- lips should reflect the character’s natural resting shape
- preserve subtle firmness, asymmetry, or tension if present
- avoid softening into a generic relaxed or “pleasant” shape

Jaw:

- jaw should remain structurally consistent with the character’s identity
- do not relax into a softer or more idealized form

Expression quality:

- expression must be neutral but not genericized
- avoid introducing friendliness, softness, or approachability not present in the base identity
- the face should feel structurally alive, not emotionally expressive

Important:

- do not inherit expression or emotional tone from reference images
- reset to a neutral state without removing identity-defining structure
