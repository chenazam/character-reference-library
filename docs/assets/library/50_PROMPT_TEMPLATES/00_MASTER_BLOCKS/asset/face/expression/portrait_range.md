Portrait expression range:

This asset allows controlled personality expression while maintaining realism and consistency.

Allowed:

- subtle smirk
- relaxed confidence
- soft friendliness
- slight attitude conveyed through eyes or mouth

Expression intensity:

- must remain restrained and natural
- no exaggerated facial movement
- no extreme emotion

Avoid:

- wide open mouth expressions
- exaggerated smiles or laughter
- aggressive or theatrical expressions
- stylized or performative facial acting

Character alignment:

- expression should reflect the character’s personality
- must feel natural and effortless, not posed or forced

Priority:

- maintain character identity
- allow mild emotional readability
- avoid breaking consistency with the rendering system
