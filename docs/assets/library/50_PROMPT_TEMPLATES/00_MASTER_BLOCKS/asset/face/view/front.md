View:
strict front-facing head reference

Orientation:

- head facing directly forward
- no rotation to either side
- no tilt
- no upward or downward angle
- head positioned upright or only minimally tilted
- avoid pronounced head tilt or submissive angles

Facial visibility:

- both eyes fully visible
- both eyebrows fully visible
- nose and mouth centered
- facial features symmetrical in view

Gaze:

- eyes directed straight toward the camera
- pupils centered relative to eye shape
- no lateral gaze shift

Head alignment:

- neck aligned naturally under the head
- no turned or twisted head position
- no off-axis portrait pose

Constraints:

- no three-quarter facial angle
- no profile bias
- no exaggerated or stylized facial distortion

Framing constraint:

- full body must not be visible
- image must not include legs or full torso
