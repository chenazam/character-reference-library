View:
controlled three-quarter face presentation

Orientation:

- head rotated to a clear three-quarter angle
- angle must be strong enough to show facial volume clearly
- angle must not drift into near-front view
- angle must not drift into side profile

Head alignment:

- head and neck must rotate together as a single unit
- the angle of the jaw, neck, and collar alignment must match
- no independent rotation of the head relative to the torso
- the connection between jaw and neck must appear anatomically continuous
- no off-axis fashion pose
- no tilted or thrown-back head position

Facial visibility:

- both eyes visible
- far eye partially reduced by perspective but still clearly readable
- nose bridge, cheek contour, jawline, and facial planes clearly visible
- mouth fully visible and anatomically aligned

Gaze:

- eyes directed straight toward the camera
- pupils centered relative to eye shape
- no lateral gaze shift

Expression:

- neutral to slightly relaxed expression
- restrained and natural facial movement only
- avoid exaggerated emotion or strong attitude

Identity requirements:

- preserve exact facial identity from the front reference
- preserve eye shape, nose shape, mouth shape, and jaw structure
- preserve facial proportions under rotation
- avoid beautification or reinterpretation of features

Constraints:

- no dramatic expression
- no portrait-glamour pose
- no excessive turn toward camera
- no mismatch between face angle and neck connection
