Output format (strict):

- vertical portrait orientation (height greater than width)
- aspect ratio must be portrait (2:3 or 3:4)
- do NOT use square or landscape orientation

Canvas behavior:

- canvas must be vertically oriented
- height must significantly exceed width
- do not reinterpret as square or horizontal framing

Composition:

- subject centered
- tight portrait crop
- face and upper shoulders only

Priority:

- portrait orientation is mandatory
- if the image is not vertical, the result is incorrect

Framing priority (critical):

- the face must occupy ~70–80% of the vertical frame
- crop closer to the head
- reduce visible chest area
- shoulders may be partially cropped
