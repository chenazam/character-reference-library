## Framing (STANDARDIZED)

- portrait image (2:3 aspect ratio)

- head centered horizontally
- head occupies most of the frame (~65–75% of image height)

- small, consistent margin above the hair
- chin fully visible with a slightly larger margin below
- shoulders slightly visible at the bottom of the frame

- eyes positioned slightly above center (~35–40% from top)

- framing must be stable and consistent across all face reference views
