Body reference priorities:

- preserve canonical body proportions
- maintain consistent shoulder, waist, hip, torso, arm, and leg relationships
- prioritize anatomy readability and silhouette clarity
- preserve body mass distribution and overall physique
- avoid exaggerated motion or stylized distortion
- keep the body clearly readable as a reusable reference asset

Proportion clarity:

- maintain balanced head-to-body ratio
- avoid elongated limb proportions
- avoid small head relative to body

- overall proportions should read natural and grounded

Muscle definition control:

- avoid sharp or high-contrast muscle separation
- muscle structure should be suggested, not outlined

- avoid clearly segmented abdominal definition
- maintain soft, natural transitions across the torso

Silhouette clarity:

- ensure clear and readable mass distribution across the body
- major forms (torso, hips, thighs) must not collapse into a single vertical line

- avoid overly straight or flattened side profiles
- maintain distinct curvature where anatomically appropriate
