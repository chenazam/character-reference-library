Studio background behavior:

This asset uses a soft studio-style background instead of a flat color.

Background:

- subtle gradient or soft tonal variation
- low contrast
- neutral color palette

Behavior:

- background must not draw attention
- must not compete with the subject
- must remain clean and uncluttered

Avoid:

- visible textures or patterns
- environmental elements or scenery
- strong gradients or vignettes
- color casts that affect the subject

Edges:

- subject edges must remain clean and clearly readable
- no blending or fading into the background

Consistency:

- background style should remain consistent across similar asset types
