Identity priority:

- preserve exact facial identity for each character
- facial structure, eye shape, nose, mouth, and jawline must remain consistent with the character reference
- do not alter or reinterpret facial features under different lighting or angles

Scene-specific constraints:

- lighting and environment must not significantly change perceived identity
- maintain recognizable likeness even under warm, low-light, or directional lighting
- avoid softening, reshaping, or idealizing facial structure

Multi-character requirement:

- each character must remain clearly distinguishable
- do not blend or average facial features between characters
- ensure both faces remain individually recognizable at a glance

Goal:

- maintain strong facial identity while allowing natural scene rendering
