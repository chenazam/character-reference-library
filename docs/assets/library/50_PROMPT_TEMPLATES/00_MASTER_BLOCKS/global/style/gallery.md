Gallery rendering style:

This asset uses a controlled illustration rendering that balances realism and clarity.

Base style:

- grounded in realistic anatomy and material behavior
- supported by clean, visible linework
- avoids both fully painterly and fully flat rendering

Linework:

- present and clearly defined
- slightly softer than strict reference assets
- used to maintain structure and readability

Shading:

- smooth and natural transitions
- slightly softer than reference sheets
- maintain clarity of forms without harsh contrast

Materials:

- realistic but simplified
- avoid noisy textures
- maintain consistent material appearance

Depth:

- allow mild depth and form separation
- avoid cinematic lighting or dramatic contrast

Avoid:

- overly soft or airbrushed rendering
- heavy painterly stylization
- harsh dramatic lighting
- excessive texture detail

Consistency:

- must remain visually compatible with reference assets
- must preserve character identity and recognizability

Priority:

- clarity
- consistency
- natural presentation
