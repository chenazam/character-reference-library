Style:

- high-quality realistic scene rendering
- naturalistic human appearance with believable materials, lighting, and depth
- realistic proportions and facial structure
- visually cohesive scene rendering rather than reference-sheet presentation

Rendering characteristics:

- soft, natural transitions in light and shadow
- believable skin, fabric, hair, and environmental materials
- realistic depth, atmosphere, and spatial separation
- detailed but natural surface rendering without diagrammatic sharpness

Scene rendering priorities:

- preserve character identity while integrating the characters naturally into the scene
- maintain believable interaction between characters, clothing, props, and environment
- prioritize realism, mood, and visual cohesion over asset-style readability
- allow natural variation in lighting, perspective, and rendering appropriate to the scene

Consistency requirements:

- the character must still read as the same person
- preserve recognizable facial structure, hairstyle, body proportions, and overall silhouette
- maintain internal scene consistency in lighting, color, and material rendering
- ensure all characters feel like they belong in the same environment and image

Avoid:

- reference-sheet or technical illustration presentation
- overly simplified lighting intended only for readability
- excessively hard contour emphasis or diagrammatic linework
- painterly abstraction or heavy stylization
- anime, comic, or cartoon stylization
- plastic skin, artificial airbrushing, or over-smoothed materials

Goal:

- preserve character identity within a believable, visually cohesive, realistic scene
- balance realism, attractiveness, and narrative atmosphere without drifting into stylization
