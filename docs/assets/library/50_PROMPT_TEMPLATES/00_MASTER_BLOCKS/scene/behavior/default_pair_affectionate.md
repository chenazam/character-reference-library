Default affectionate two-character behavior:

This scene contains two distinct characters with a soft, comfortable, and mutually warm connection.

Interaction baseline:

- interaction should feel naturally affectionate, but not highly specialized or intense
- closeness should read as mutual, easy, and unforced
- do not imply extreme tension, dominance, or highly charged intimacy unless explicitly instructed elsewhere
- do not invent a highly specific relationship dynamic beyond gentle affection and familiarity

Connection quality:

- relaxed physical and emotional comfort
- easy familiarity
- light attentiveness toward each other
- natural warmth rather than dramatic chemistry

Pose behavior:

- allow close proximity and light contact
- contact should feel casual and believable rather than staged
- avoid mirrored posing unless explicitly requested
- avoid highly theatrical, exaggerated, or romance-cover-style composition

Contact examples:

- light touch at arm, shoulder, upper back, or hand
- relaxed leaning into shared space
- standing or sitting close without tension
- subtle body orientation toward each other

Expression behavior:

- soft, warm, or lightly engaged expressions
- natural eye contact is allowed, but not required
- expressions should remain understated and believable
- avoid intense staring, exaggerated flirtation, or highly dramatic emotion

Character preservation:

- each character must remain visually distinct
- preserve each character’s facial structure, body proportions, clothing logic, and movement behavior
- do not let one character drift toward the proportions or styling of the other
- do not merge silhouettes or obscure identity through excessive overlap

Composition behavior:

- both characters should remain individually readable
- allow moderate overlap only when identity, anatomy, and clothing remain clear
- preserve limb clarity and body readability
- framing should feel open, natural, and balanced

Default emotional tone:

- warm
- calm
- affectionate
- familiar
- unforced
