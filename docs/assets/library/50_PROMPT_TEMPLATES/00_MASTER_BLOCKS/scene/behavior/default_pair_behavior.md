Default two-character behavior:

This scene contains two distinct characters who must remain clearly separable and individually readable.

Interaction baseline:

- use neutral, non-romantic, non-specialized two-character staging unless additional pair-specific instructions are provided
- do not invent relationship-specific dynamics
- do not imply intimacy, tension, or familiarity unless explicitly instructed elsewhere
- do not merge or average their identities, body types, or styling

Character separation:

- each character must remain visually distinct
- preserve each character’s own facial structure, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing logic, and movement behavior
- do not let one character drift toward the proportions or styling of the other
- maintain clear silhouette separation whenever possible

Pose behavior:

- use natural, everyday co-presence
- avoid mirrored posing unless explicitly requested
- avoid overly theatrical staging
- avoid contact that obscures anatomy, clothing, or identity readability
- if both characters are standing, keep posture neutral and grounded

Composition behavior:

- both characters should be readable as separate individuals
- avoid overlap that hides critical body or face information
- preserve limb clarity and body readability
- maintain balanced framing across both characters

Default emotional tone:

- neutral
- calm
- situationally natural
- unforced

If no pair-specific block is provided, prioritize clarity, separation, and character consistency over chemistry or interaction.
