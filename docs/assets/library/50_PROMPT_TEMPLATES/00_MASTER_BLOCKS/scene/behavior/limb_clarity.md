Limb clarity:

- each visible limb must have a clear anatomical origin
- hands must be visibly connected to their respective arms
- avoid extra or duplicated hands, arms, or fingers

- when characters overlap:
- limb ownership must remain unambiguous
- avoid partially hidden limbs that cannot be clearly traced

Contact constraints:

- if one character is touching the other:
- limit contact to clearly defined points (e.g. one hand on waist)
- avoid multiple overlapping or stacked hands in the same region

Avoid:

- extra hands or arms
- disembodied or floating hands
- overlapping limbs that cannot be visually resolved
