Candid behavior:

- the character should not appear aware of the camera
- avoid direct eye contact with the viewer unless explicitly required
- gaze should be directed naturally within the scene (e.g. at surroundings, objects, or off into space)
- body posture should feel unposed and incidental rather than deliberately presented
- avoid symmetrical or centered posing unless justified by the scene

Framing behavior:

- avoid overly clean or studio-like composition
- allow slight asymmetry in framing
- background should remain part of the scene rather than purely a backdrop
- depth of field should be natural and not excessively isolate the subject

Goal:

- the image should feel like a captured moment rather than a posed photograph
