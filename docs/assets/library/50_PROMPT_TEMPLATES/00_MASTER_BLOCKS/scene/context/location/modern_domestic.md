## Location — Modern Domestic

The scene takes place in a contemporary indoor residential environment.

The space reflects a present-day setting with modern materials, furnishings, and layout. Surfaces, objects, and architecture should read as current and realistic rather than stylized or period-specific.

Examples include:

- living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, or hallways in a modern home or apartment
- clean, functional interiors with believable furniture and everyday objects

Visual characteristics:

- contemporary furniture and layout
- neutral or natural materials (wood, fabric, metal, glass)
- soft, natural or practical indoor lighting

Constraints:

- no medieval, historical, or fantasy architecture
- no castle-like structures, stone halls, or period interiors
- avoid exaggerated or stylized set design

The environment should feel grounded, lived-in, and appropriate to present-day life.
