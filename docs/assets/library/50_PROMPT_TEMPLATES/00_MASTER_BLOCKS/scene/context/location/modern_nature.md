## Location — Modern Natural Environment

The scene takes place in a natural outdoor environment within a present-day context.

The setting should feel grounded and realistic, without stylization or fantasy elements.

Examples include:

- forests, fields, gardens, parks, or trails
- natural landscapes with subtle signs of modern presence if applicable

Visual characteristics:

- natural lighting and organic materials
- realistic terrain, vegetation, and atmosphere
- minimal artificial structure unless contextually appropriate

Constraints:

- no fantasy landscapes or stylized environments
- no historical or medieval structures unless explicitly specified
- avoid exaggerated or surreal visual elements

The environment should feel like a real, contemporary outdoor space.
