## Location — Modern Urban

The scene takes place in a contemporary urban environment.

The setting should reflect a modern city context with realistic architecture, infrastructure, and spatial density.

Examples include:

- streets, sidewalks, building exteriors, alleys, rooftops
- urban interiors such as lobbies or shared spaces

Visual characteristics:

- modern buildings, materials, and construction
- concrete, glass, metal, and contemporary design language
- natural or city-based lighting conditions

Constraints:

- no historical, medieval, or fantasy architecture
- no castle-like or period-inspired structures
- avoid stylized or anachronistic environments

The environment should clearly read as present-day urban space.
