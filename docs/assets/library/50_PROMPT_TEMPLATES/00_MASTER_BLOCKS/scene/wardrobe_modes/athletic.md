## Wardrobe Mode — Athletic

Align the outfit with movement, function, and physical activity.

- prioritize lightweight, breathable, movement-friendly clothing
- silhouettes should support mobility and flexibility
- fits may be slightly closer to the body but not restrictive
- materials should feel practical and performance-oriented

Avoid:

- heavy fabrics or layered styling
- decorative or purely aesthetic elements
- anything that limits movement or feels impractical
