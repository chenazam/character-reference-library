## Wardrobe Mode — Elevated

Refine the outfit toward a more polished and intentional presentation.

- silhouettes remain clean but slightly more structured
- fabrics appear higher quality or more refined
- color combinations should feel deliberate and balanced

- styling becomes more composed without losing naturalness
- details should be subtle but intentional

Avoid:

- overly formal or rigid clothing
- heavy layering or complex styling
- anything that feels like a fashion editorial or costume
