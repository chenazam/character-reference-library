## Wardrobe Mode — Intimate

Shift the outfit toward softness, closeness, and personal space.

- favor minimal or lightweight clothing
- silhouettes may be slightly looser or more natural
- allow subtle exposure of skin where appropriate
- fabrics should feel soft, tactile, and comfortable

- reduce visual complexity and styling effort
- colors may lean warm, soft, or slightly desaturated

Avoid:

- heavy structure or layering
- bold or attention-seeking styling
- anything that feels performative or outward-facing
