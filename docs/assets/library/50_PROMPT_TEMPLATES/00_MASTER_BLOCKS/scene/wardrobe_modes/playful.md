## Wardrobe Mode — Playful

Increase lightness, spontaneity, and expressiveness in the outfit.

- allow slightly more relaxed or revealing cuts where appropriate
- prioritize movement-friendly silhouettes
- introduce warmth or softness in color where possible
- styling should feel effortless, slightly teasing, and unforced

Avoid:

- heavy layering or rigid structure
- overly curated or editorial styling
- anything that feels stiff or overly controlled
