Create a clean, standardized **front-facing face reference** for a character design pipeline.

---

## Purpose

This image establishes the **canonical facial identity** of the character.

It must:

- strongly reflect the intended appearance
- normalize the character into a clean, reusable format
- serve as the **primary identity source** for all downstream assets

## Reference Interpretation (Casting Step)

Use the provided image references to define the character’s facial structure.

Requirements:

- combine features from all references into one coherent identity
- preserve overall proportions, bone structure, and distinctive traits
- maintain strong visual resemblance to the references
- do not replicate a specific real person exactly
- resolve differences between references into a single consistent face
- prioritize structural consistency over photographic detail

Important:
The output must feel like a **unified character derived from the references**, not a direct copy of any one individual.

---

## View & Pose

- perfectly front-facing view
- head centered and level
- neutral expression
- eyes looking straight forward
- mouth relaxed and closed
- no head tilt (no pitch, yaw, or roll deviation from true front)

---

## Framing

- crop from the top of the hair to the base of the neck
- do not include shoulders or torso
- ears must remain visible where applicable
- minimize empty space above the hair and below the chin
- maintain consistent vertical centering of the head

---

## Aspect Ratio & Composition

- portrait orientation with a 3:4 aspect ratio
- the head should occupy approximately 70–80% of the image height
- avoid wide portrait framing (do not use 2:3 or similar ratios)
- composition should feel vertically contained, not spacious

The image must not resemble a photographic head-and-shoulders portrait.
Avoid common DSLR portrait framing.

---

## Lighting

- soft, even lighting
- no harsh shadows
- no directional lighting bias
- full facial features clearly visible

---

## Background

- uniform light neutral background (consistent across all generations)
- no gradients, no textures

---

## Appearance

- natural skin texture
- clean grooming
- no stylization
- no painterly effects
- no heavy contrast or cinematic grading

---

## Clothing

- simple neutral top (e.g. t-shirt)
- no logos, patterns, or accessories

---

## Rendering Style

- realistic or softly stylized realism
- consistent and clean rendering
- no exaggerated features
- no anime or cartoon style

---

## Output Requirements

- single image
- front-facing only
- high clarity and readability
- clean, centered composition
- consistent proportions
- portrait-oriented image in 3:4 ratio
- tightly framed head-focused composition

---

## Important Constraints

- no text or labels
- no multiple angles
- no expression variations
- no background elements
- no props or accessories

---

## Pipeline Rule

This image becomes the **canonical facial identity** of the character.

All subsequent assets must be derived from this image —
not from the original photo references.
