## View (PROFILE — STRICT)

- head rotated to a true 90° profile view

- no visible rotation toward or away from the viewer

- only one eye visible

- the far eye must not be visible

- the nose must fully define the facial silhouette

- the jawline and chin must be clearly readable in profile

- no head tilt (no up/down angle)

---

## Identity Preservation (CRITICAL)

[IDENTITY_PRESERVATION_BLOCK]

- the task is to rotate the same head into a profile view
- do not reinterpret or redesign facial features

---

## Profile Integrity (CRITICAL)

- the face must be a true profile, not a three-quarter view

- the bridge and tip of the nose must be clearly defined in silhouette

- lips must follow the natural facial contour in profile

- chin and jawline must match the front view structure
