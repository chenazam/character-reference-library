## View (THREE-QUARTER — STRICT)

- head rotated approximately 40–45° from front view
- both eyes visible
- far eye partially occluded naturally by perspective
- no head tilt (no up/down angle)
- no exaggerated rotation beyond a natural three-quarter view
- the face must clearly read as a rotated version of the front view, not a different face

---

## Identity Preservation (CRITICAL)

[IDENTITY_PRESERVATION_BLOCK]

- the task is to rotate the same head into a three-quarter view
- do not reinterpret or redesign facial features

---

## Angle Integrity (CRITICAL)

- the face must remain clearly distinct from a full side profile
- both eyes must remain visible
- the nose must not align with the outer facial contour
- the far cheek must remain partially visible
