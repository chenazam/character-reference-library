## Anatomy Side Reference

Create a clean, neutral full-body profile view anatomy reference image for a character design pipeline.

This asset defines the character’s body depth, posture, and silhouette in profile and must be highly consistent and reusable.

## View (PROFILE — STRICT)

- true 90-degree profile view

- the body is viewed exactly from the side

- no rotation toward or away from the viewer

- no three-quarter angle

- only one side of the body visible

- the far side of the body must not be visible

---

## Pose (PROFILE — NEUTRAL)

- neutral anatomical standing pose
- upright posture with no lean or tilt
- shoulders and hips level and aligned
- legs straight, hip-width apart, weight evenly distributed
- arms hanging vertically with elbows fully extended
- upper arms slightly separated from the torso with a consistent gap
- hands slightly away from thighs, palms facing inward
- fingers extended and relaxed
- perfect left-right symmetry
- identical pose across all views, only rotated in space

---

## Posture & Alignment (CRITICAL)

- head aligned vertically over the torso

- ear aligned approximately over the shoulder in profile

- spine in a natural neutral alignment

- no forward head posture

- no exaggerated arch in the lower back

- pelvis in a neutral position (not tilted forward or backward)

- knees fully extended (not bent)

- abdomen relaxed (not flexed or held in)

---

## Structure & Proportions (CRITICAL)

- preserve the character’s exact body proportions and physical traits

- maintain:
  - chest depth
  - abdominal projection
  - glute projection
  - back curvature
  - leg shape and thickness
  - arm thickness and length

- preserve the natural distribution of:
  - muscle mass
  - body fat
  - softness or definition

- do not idealize or standardize the body

- do not increase muscle definition or reduce body fat

- if the character has soft features, preserve softness exactly

- if the character has asymmetry, preserve asymmetry

- if the character has non-athletic proportions, preserve them exactly

---

## Silhouette Clarity (CRITICAL)

- the body outline must be clean and clearly readable in profile

- the contour of:
  - chest
  - abdomen
  - glutes
  - thighs
    must be clearly distinguishable

## Clothing (STANDARDIZED — FILTER SAFE)

- bare torso (no shirt)

- fitted compression shorts
  - mid-thigh length
  - snug fit to clearly show body shape
  - no folds, no loose fabric

- shorts must be in a light to mid-gray or desaturated blue or green

- the color must be clearly visible (not black or very dark)

- the shorts must remain clearly distinguishable from shadows and background

- no additional clothing, accessories, or props

---

## Framing (STANDARDIZED)

- portrait image (2:3 aspect ratio)

- full body visible from head to feet

- no cropping of head or feet

- character centered horizontally

- small margin above the head

- small margin below the feet

- consistent scale across all anatomy panels

---

## Rendering (NEUTRAL)

- soft, even lighting

- no strong shadows

- plain, neutral background

- natural skin tones

- no stylization or painterly effects
