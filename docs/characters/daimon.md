<p>Daimon is an extremely tall, massively built man with a dense muscular frame and a commanding physical presence. His broad shoulders, thick limbs, and square facial structure create a powerful silhouette that reads rugged, stoic, and physically formidable.</p>

<p>His aesthetic is dark, practical, and utilitarian, built around leather, heavy materials, restrained color palettes, and a hardened masculine edge. Even at rest, he feels imposing and controlled, with a presence defined more by contained force than overt motion.</p>
